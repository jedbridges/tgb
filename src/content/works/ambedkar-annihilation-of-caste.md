synopsis: >-
  The text of a speech Ambedkar was invited to give to a Hindu reform society in Lahore in 1936 and which the organisers cancelled when they read it. He argues that caste is not a division of labour but a division of labourers, that it has destroyed Hindu society's capacity for fellow feeling, and that reform that stops short of intermarriage and the destruction of the religious sanction for caste is useless. Since the sanction lies in the shastras, the shastras must be rejected. Later editions added his exchange with Gandhi.
