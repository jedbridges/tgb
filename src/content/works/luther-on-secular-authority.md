synopsis: >-
  Written for Duke John of Saxony, this treatise sets out Luther's doctrine of the two kingdoms. God governs the world in two ways: through the gospel, which rules believers inwardly and needs no sword, and through temporal authority, which restrains the wicked by law and force and is a genuine divine office rather than a necessary evil. Luther asks how far a Christian may hold such an office, and how far subjects must obey. Rulers may not command belief or seize books, because the soul is beyond their reach; where they overstep, subjects should refuse to comply but not rebel.
