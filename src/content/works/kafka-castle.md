  Kafka's last, unfinished novel, published by Max Brod two years after his death. A man known only as K. arrives one winter night in a village governed by a castle on the hill and claims to have been summoned as a land surveyor. The castle neither confirms nor denies this. K. spends the novel trying to reach the official Klamm, taking up with the barmaid Frieda, and being drawn into the village's fear of the authorities, without ever getting nearer the castle itself.
