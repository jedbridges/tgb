synopsis: >-
  An unfinished treatise on the nature of war, assembled from Clausewitz's manuscripts by his widow after his death from cholera. Its famous claim is that war is the continuation of policy by other means, so that military action must always serve political ends. Clausewitz describes war as a trinity of violence, chance and reason; distinguishes absolute war in theory from real war, hedged by friction; and analyses strategy, the culminating point of the attack, the superiority of defence and the moral forces that decide battles.
