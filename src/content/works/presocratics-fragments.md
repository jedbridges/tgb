synopsis: >-
  The surviving words of the Greek thinkers before Socrates, preserved as quotations and reports in later authors. They include the Milesians Thales, Anaximander and Anaximenes, who sought the single stuff of the world; Pythagoras and his school; Heraclitus on flux and the logos; Parmenides' poem arguing that what is cannot change; Zeno's paradoxes; Empedocles' four elements; Anaxagoras' mind; and the atomists Leucippus and Democritus. Together they mark the beginning of natural science and metaphysics.
