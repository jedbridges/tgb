synopsis: >-
  Schiller's last completed play, a drama in five acts about the legendary founding of the Swiss Confederation. Under the tyranny of the Habsburg governor Gessler, the men of three forest cantons swear an oath of resistance on the Rütli meadow. The hunter Tell, who has kept apart from politics, refuses to bow to Gessler's hat on a pole, is forced to shoot an apple from his son's head, and later ambushes and kills Gessler on the road to Küssnacht. The cantons rise, the castles fall, and Tell is hailed as the liberator.
