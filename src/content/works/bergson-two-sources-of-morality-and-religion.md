synopsis: >-
  Bergson's last book distinguishes two kinds of morality and two kinds of religion. Closed morality is the pressure of social obligation that holds a group together against outsiders; open morality is the call of exceptional individuals, saints and heroes, to a love of all humanity. Static religion is the myth-making function that defends society against the dissolving power of intelligence; dynamic religion is mysticism, contact with the creative source of life. The closing chapter considers whether mechanised humanity can recover the mystical impulse.
