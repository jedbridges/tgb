synopsis: >-
  An appeal to the German princes and knights to reform the church themselves, since Rome will not. Luther begins by knocking down what he calls the three walls the papacy hides behind: that clergy outrank laity, that only the pope may interpret scripture, and that only the pope may call a council. Against the first he argues that every baptised Christian is a priest. He then lists grievances, from papal taxation and the traffic in benefices to clerical celibacy and the state of the universities, and proposes concrete measures for each.
