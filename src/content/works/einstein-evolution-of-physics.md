synopsis: >-
  A history of physical ideas for the general reader, written with Leopold Infeld and using no mathematics. It follows the rise of the mechanical view from Galileo and Newton, its decline as the field concept emerged from the work of Faraday and Maxwell, the puzzles about light and ether that led to relativity, and the strange evidence that forced the quantum theory. The authors present physics as a detective story in which each solved case raises a harder one.
