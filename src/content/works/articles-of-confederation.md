synopsis: >-
  The first constitution of the United States, adopted by the Continental Congress in November 1777 and in force from 1781 to 1789. Its thirteen articles create a league of friendship among sovereign states with a single-chamber Congress in which each state has one vote. Congress may make war and treaties but cannot tax, regulate commerce or compel the states, and amendments require unanimity. Its weaknesses drove the calling of the Philadelphia Convention of 1787.
