synopsis: >-
  Twelve years after the Origin, Darwin applies his theory to human beings. The first part argues that humans descend from an ape-like ancestor, that our mental and moral faculties differ from those of animals in degree rather than kind, and that the moral sense grows out of social instincts. The second and longest part develops the theory of sexual selection, in which mates rather than environments choose, and surveys its effects across insects, birds and mammals before applying it to human races and the differences between the sexes.
