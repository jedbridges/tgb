synopsis: >-
  An opera in two acts, music by Mozart and libretto by Lorenzo Da Ponte, first performed in Prague. The nobleman Don Giovanni, with his servant Leporello, seduces and abandons women across Spain; in the opening scene he kills the Commendatore, father of Donna Anna, whom he has assaulted. Pursued by Anna, her fiancé Ottavio, the abandoned Elvira and the peasant Zerlina, he mocks a statue of the dead man and invites it to dinner. The statue comes, and drags him unrepentant into hell.
