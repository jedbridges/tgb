synopsis: >-
  The two books of Samuel narrate Israel's transition from judges to kings. The prophet Samuel reluctantly anoints Saul as first king at the people's demand, then anoints the shepherd David when Saul disobeys. David kills Goliath, befriends Saul's son Jonathan, flees Saul's jealousy and becomes king of a united Israel with Jerusalem as its capital. His adultery with Bathsheba and the murder of her husband bring the prophet Nathan's rebuke and a family catastrophe of rape, fratricide and the rebellion and death of his son Absalom.
