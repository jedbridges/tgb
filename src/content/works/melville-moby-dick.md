synopsis: >-
  Ishmael, a restless schoolteacher, signs on to the Nantucket whaler Pequod with his new friend, the tattooed harpooner Queequeg. The ship's captain, Ahab, has lost a leg to a white sperm whale, Moby Dick, and reveals that the voyage's true purpose is revenge. The narrative alternates the hunt with chapters on the anatomy, history and meaning of whales, the whiteness of the whale, and the work of whaling. Ahab overrules his first mate Starbuck, ignores omens and other ships, and finds the whale, which destroys the Pequod. Only Ishmael survives, floating on Queequeg's coffin.
