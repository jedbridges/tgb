synopsis: >-
  Written in Judeo-Arabic as a long letter to a former student, the Guide is addressed to readers who know both philosophy and the Law and cannot see how they fit. Part One reads the physical language of scripture, God's hand, place and speech, as figurative, and argues that nothing positive can be said of God at all, only what he is not. Part Two examines the Aristotelian proofs of God and the eternity of the world, defends creation as possible rather than demonstrated, and explains prophecy. Part Three turns to providence, the book of Job, evil, and the reasons behind the commandments.
