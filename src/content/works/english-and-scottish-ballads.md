  Anonymous narrative songs passed on orally in England and Scotland, written down from the late Middle Ages and collected in bulk by Percy, Scott and Francis James Child in the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries. In short stanzas with refrains and abrupt leaps, they tell of murdered lovers, drowned sailors, revenants, border raids and outlaws.
