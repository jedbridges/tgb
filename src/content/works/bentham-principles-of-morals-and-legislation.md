synopsis: >-
  The founding text of utilitarianism. Bentham opens by declaring that nature has placed mankind under two sovereign masters, pain and pleasure, and that the principle of utility, approving every action according to its tendency to increase or diminish happiness, is the only defensible standard for morals and law. He sets out a calculus for measuring pleasures and pains by intensity, duration and other dimensions, classifies the kinds of pleasure and of motive, and applies the whole to the theory of punishment and the division of offences.
