synopsis: >-
  Six years of letters between the exiled Palatine princess and Descartes, begun when she asked him the question his Meditations had left unanswered: how an unextended thinking soul can move a body. His replies never satisfy her, and the objection has been repeated ever since. The exchange then turns to how to live. Elisabeth describes her own illnesses, her family's misfortunes and her difficulty in following the advice to control the passions by thought, and presses Descartes on Seneca's account of the happy life, on the value of the passions, and on the tension between free will and divine providence.
