synopsis: >-
  Delivered on 4 March 1865, six weeks before Lincoln's assassination and with the Civil War nearly won, this speech of some 700 words declines to celebrate. Lincoln recalls that both sides read the same Bible and prayed to the same God, names slavery as the cause of the war, and suggests that God may be exacting from the whole nation, North and South, payment for the wealth piled up by two hundred and fifty years of unrequited toil. He closes by urging the country to finish the work with malice toward none and charity for all.
