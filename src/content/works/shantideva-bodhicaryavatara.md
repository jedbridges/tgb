synopsis: >-
  A poem in ten chapters by a monk of the university of Nalanda on the path of the bodhisattva, the one who vows to attain enlightenment for the sake of all beings. It begins by praising the awakening mind, confesses faults and generates the resolve, then treats the perfections in turn, vigilance, patience, energy, meditation and wisdom. The chapter on patience argues against anger; the meditation chapter teaches the exchange of self and other; the ninth chapter is a compressed philosophical defence of the Madhyamaka doctrine of emptiness. It has been the most read text of Tibetan Buddhism for centuries.
