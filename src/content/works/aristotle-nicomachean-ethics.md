  Aristotle's lecture course on how a human life goes well. He argues that happiness is activity in accordance with virtue, and examines the moral virtues (courage, temperance, generosity, justice) as means between extremes, the intellectual virtues, weakness of will, pleasure, and friendship. The ten books close by asking whether the best life is political or contemplative.
