synopsis: >-
  A short treatise by the young Athanasius, companion to his Against the Gentiles, on why God became man. Human beings were made from nothing and given a share in the Word's own life; turning from God, they fell back toward the nothing they came from, and death and corruption followed. No repentance could reverse corruption, and God would not simply let his work perish, so the Word took a human body in order to die in it and destroy death from inside. Athanasius then answers Jewish and Greek objections, arguing from prophecy and from the visible collapse of idolatry.
