synopsis: >-
  Bergson's doctoral thesis and first book. He argues that psychologists go wrong by treating states of mind as quantities that can be measured, and that time as we live it, which he calls duration, is not a line of separate instants but a flowing multiplicity in which moments interpenetrate. The spatialised clock time of science is a useful fiction. The final chapter turns this against determinism, claiming that a free act is one that expresses the whole self and cannot be predicted because it is not a sum of parts.
