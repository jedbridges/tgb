synopsis: >-
  Eighty-five newspaper essays published in New York in 1787 and 1788 under the name Publius to persuade the state to ratify the proposed Constitution. Hamilton wrote the majority, Madison about a third and Jay five. They argue for a strong union against the failures of the Articles of Confederation, defend the new government's powers over taxation and the military, and explain its structure. Madison's No. 10 argues that a large republic controls the mischiefs of faction, and No. 51 that ambition must be made to counteract ambition; Hamilton's No. 78 defends judicial review.
