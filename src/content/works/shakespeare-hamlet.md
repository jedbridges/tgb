synopsis: >-
  Prince Hamlet returns to Elsinore to find his father dead and his mother married to his uncle Claudius, who now holds the throne. His father's ghost tells him he was murdered by Claudius and demands revenge. Hamlet feigns madness, stages a play to test the ghost's story, kills the courtier Polonius by mistake, and is shipped to England. He returns to a poisoned duel that kills Gertrude, Laertes, Claudius and himself.
