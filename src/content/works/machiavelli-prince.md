synopsis: >-
  A short handbook on how to acquire and keep a state, written by a dismissed Florentine official and dedicated to Lorenzo de' Medici. Machiavelli classifies principalities, discusses armies and mercenaries, and then, in the famous central chapters, argues that a prince must learn how not to be good, be feared rather than loved, and keep faith only when it pays. Cesare Borgia is the model; fortune is a river to be dammed; the last chapter calls for Italy's liberation.
