synopsis: >-
  A closet drama modelled on Greek tragedy, published with Paradise Regained. Samson, blinded and enslaved by the Philistines after Dalila betrayed him, is visited in turn by his father Manoa, by Dalila herself, and by the giant Harapha, and works through despair, self-reproach and anger to a renewed sense of divine calling. Summoned to perform at a Philistine festival, he pulls down the temple on his captors and himself, an act reported by a messenger to the chorus and to Manoa.
