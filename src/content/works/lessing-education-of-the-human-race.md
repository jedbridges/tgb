synopsis: >-
  A short treatise in one hundred numbered paragraphs, published anonymously in the last year of Lessing's life. It proposes that revelation is to the human race what education is to the individual, giving it early what it could have reached by reason but only much later. The Old Testament was a primer for a childish people, the New Testament a second primer for its youth, and a third age of reason is coming in which humanity will do good for its own sake. Lessing closes with a hint that souls may return to earth more than once.
