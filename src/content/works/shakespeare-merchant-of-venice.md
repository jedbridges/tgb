synopsis: >-
  To finance his friend Bassanio's courtship of the heiress Portia, the merchant Antonio borrows from the Jewish moneylender Shylock, whom he has long abused, and agrees in jest to forfeit a pound of his flesh if he defaults. Bassanio wins Portia by choosing the right casket, but Antonio's ships are lost and Shylock demands the bond. Portia, disguised as a lawyer, defeats Shylock in court on a technicality; he is stripped of his wealth and forced to convert.
