synopsis: >-
  A novel in seven volumes, three of them published after Proust's death, narrated by a man looking back on his life from childhood in Combray to old age in Paris after the First World War. Around him move the Swanns, the Guermantes, the Verdurins, the Baron de Charlus and Albertine, whom the narrator imprisons in his flat and loses. Its subjects are jealousy, snobbery, homosexuality, the Dreyfus affair, the decay of the aristocracy and the war, and above all time. In the last volume a series of involuntary memories reveals to the narrator the book he must write, which is the one just read.
