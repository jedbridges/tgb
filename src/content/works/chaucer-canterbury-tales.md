synopsis: >-
  A company of some thirty pilgrims, from a knight and a prioress to a miller and a pardoner, meet at the Tabard Inn in Southwark on their way to the shrine of Thomas Becket at Canterbury and agree to tell stories on the road. The General Prologue describes each of them. The tales that follow range from chivalric romance and saint's life to bawdy fabliau, beast fable and sermon, and the tellers quarrel, interrupt and answer one another. Chaucer left the work unfinished at his death, with twenty-four tales of the planned hundred and more.
