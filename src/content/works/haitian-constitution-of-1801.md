synopsis: >-
  The constitution Toussaint Louverture had drawn up for Saint-Domingue in 1801, after he had taken control of the whole island while still nominally under French rule. Its seventy-seven articles abolish slavery forever, declare all men free and French and equal before the law, make Catholicism the sole religion, bind labourers to plantations under a regime of compulsory work, and name Louverture governor for life with the right to choose his successor. Napoleon took it as a declaration of independence and sent an army the next year.
