  Gregers Werle, a zealot for truth, returns home and discovers that his old friend Hjalmar Ekdal's comfortable family life rests on lies arranged by Gregers' father: Hjalmar's wife Gina was the elder Werle's mistress, and their daughter Hedvig may be his. Gregers insists on telling Hjalmar everything, believing it will found a true marriage. Instead Hjalmar rejects Hedvig, and the girl, wanting to prove her love, shoots herself in the attic where the family keeps a wounded wild duck.
