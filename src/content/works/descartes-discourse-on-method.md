synopsis: >-
  Descartes introduces his new philosophy by telling the story of his own education and disillusionment with it. Part two states the four rules of his method: accept nothing that is not clearly evident, divide problems into parts, proceed from the simplest to the most complex, and review until nothing is left out. Part four gives the first published statement of the cogito and the proofs of God and the soul. The last parts defend the mechanical account of the body, argue that animals are machines while humans have reason and language, and explain why he is publishing at all.
