  An Icelandic prose saga retelling in order the poems of the heroic Edda. It follows the Volsung family from Odin's descendant Volsung through Sigmund, who draws the god's sword from a tree, to his son Sigurd, who kills the dragon Fafnir, wins its cursed gold, wakes the valkyrie Brynhild from her enchanted sleep, and then, drugged into forgetting her, marries Gudrun and wins Brynhild for Gudrun's brother Gunnar. Brynhild has Sigurd murdered and kills herself; Gudrun's later marriage to Atli ends with the slaughter of her brothers and her revenge on her husband.
