synopsis: >-
  Johnson's Dictionary, compiled almost single-handedly over nine years and published in two folio volumes in 1755, defines some 42,000 words and illustrates them with around 114,000 quotations from English writers, chiefly of the previous two centuries. Its Preface describes the labour and its limits and reflects on how a living language resists being fixed. Many definitions are memorable for their wit or prejudice, such as those for lexicographer, oats and patron. It remained the standard English dictionary until the Oxford English Dictionary.
