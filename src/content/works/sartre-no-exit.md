synopsis: >-
  A one-act play. Three people are shown into a windowless Second Empire drawing room with no mirrors and the lights permanently on: Garcin, a journalist shot for desertion, Inez, a postal clerk who drove her lover to suicide, and Estelle, a society woman who killed her baby. They wait for a torturer and gradually work out that there isn't one, because each of them is equipped to torment the other two. Each needs another's judgement to know who they are, and none will give it honestly. Garcin's conclusion is the line the play is remembered for.
