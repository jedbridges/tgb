synopsis: >-
  A chronicle play in six scenes and an epilogue about Joan of Arc, written three years after her canonisation. Joan, a farm girl who hears voices, talks her way to the Dauphin, raises the siege of Orléans and crowns him at Reims, then is captured, tried by the Inquisition for heresy and burned in 1431. Shaw presents her judges as reasonable men defending Church and feudal order against a Protestant and a nationalist before her time. The epilogue brings the dead and living together to ask whether the world is ready for its saints.
