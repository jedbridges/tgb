synopsis: >-
  A Latin treatise in three books arguing that the world needs a single universal ruler. Book one holds that peace is the condition of human flourishing and that only one supreme monarch can guarantee it. Book two argues that the Roman people acquired their empire by right, not by force. Book three, the most controversial, argues that the emperor's authority comes directly from God and not through the pope. The book was condemned and publicly burned after Dante's death.
