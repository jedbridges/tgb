synopsis: >-
  The short introduction Luther wrote for Romans in his German New Testament of 1522, and the best brief statement of his theology. He defines the key words of the epistle in turn: law, sin, grace, faith, righteousness, flesh and spirit. Sin is not just the wrong act but the unbelieving heart behind it; the law cannot cure it and only exposes it; faith is a living, restless confidence that produces good works without being asked. Luther calls Romans the purest gospel and says a Christian should know it by heart. Read aloud in London in 1738, the preface converted John Wesley.
