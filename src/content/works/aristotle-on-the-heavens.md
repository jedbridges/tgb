synopsis: >-
  Aristotle's cosmology. Books I and II argue that the heavens are made of a fifth element, aether, which moves naturally in a circle and cannot be generated or destroyed, that the universe is finite, unique and eternal, and that the earth is a sphere at rest at its centre. Books III and IV turn to the four sublunary elements, their natural motions up and down, and the meaning of heavy and light. This was the physics of the cosmos until Copernicus and Galileo.
