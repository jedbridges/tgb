synopsis: >-
  Einstein's own popular account of his two theories, written for readers with school mathematics. The first part explains special relativity through trains, embankments and lightning flashes, the constancy of the speed of light, the relativity of simultaneity and the equivalence of mass and energy. The second part introduces general relativity through the equivalence of gravitation and acceleration and the idea of curved space-time, and the third considers the shape of the universe as a whole.
