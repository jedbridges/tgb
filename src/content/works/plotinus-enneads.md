synopsis: >-
  The collected treatises of Plotinus, arranged after his death by his pupil Porphyry into six groups of nine. They describe a reality that descends from the One, beyond being and thought, through Intellect, where the Platonic forms live, to Soul, which animates the sensible world, and set out how the soul can turn back and ascend to union with its source. Individual treatises treat beauty, the descent of the soul, time and eternity, providence, evil as the absence of good, and the virtues.
