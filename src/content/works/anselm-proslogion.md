synopsis: >-
  Written at the abbey of Bec and first called Faith Seeking Understanding, the Proslogion is a prayer that contains an argument. Anselm asks God to let him understand what he already believes, and proposes a single proof that needs no premises drawn from the world. God is that than which nothing greater can be conceived; a being that exists only in the mind would be less great than one that exists in reality; therefore the fool who says in his heart there is no God is contradicting himself. The later chapters draw out the divine attributes and end in praise.
