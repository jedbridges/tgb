synopsis: >-
  On his way to answer the indictment against him, Socrates meets Euthyphro, a self-styled religious expert who is prosecuting his own father for the death of a labourer. Socrates asks him to define piety so that he can use it in his own defence. Euthyphro offers several definitions, including that the pious is what the gods love, and Socrates asks whether the gods love it because it is pious or it is pious because they love it. Every answer fails, and Euthyphro hurries away.
