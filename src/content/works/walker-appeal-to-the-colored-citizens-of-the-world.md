synopsis: >-
  A pamphlet in four articles published in Boston in 1829 by a free Black clothing dealer born in North Carolina. Walker argues that American slavery is the cruellest in history, that it is sustained by enforced ignorance, by a corrupted Christianity and by the colonisation scheme to remove free Blacks to Africa, and that God will punish the nation for it. He calls on enslaved people to resist, by force if necessary, and quotes the Declaration of Independence against its authors. Southern states banned it and put a price on his head; he died in 1830.
