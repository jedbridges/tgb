synopsis: >-
  A prose tragedy set in the Netherlands in the 1560s, at the start of the revolt against Spanish rule. Count Egmont, a popular soldier and nobleman, trusts in his own honour and the goodwill of the king, ignores the warnings of William of Orange, and stays in Brussels when the Duke of Alba arrives. Alba arrests him. His lover Klärchen tries to rouse the citizens, fails, and takes poison; Egmont, on the night before his execution, dreams of Freedom in her form and goes to his death as a call to rebellion. Beethoven wrote incidental music for it.
