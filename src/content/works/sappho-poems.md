synopsis: >-
  What survives of the lyric poet of Lesbos, who wrote around 600 BCE for the lyre and was ranked by the ancients as the tenth Muse. Of nine books only one complete poem remains, the Hymn to Aphrodite, together with a few hundred fragments quoted by later writers or recovered from papyrus, some a line long, some a single word. The poems address Aphrodite, the girls of Sappho's circle, weddings, absent friends and old age, and fragment 31, on watching the woman she loves talk to a man, is among the most imitated poems in the world.
