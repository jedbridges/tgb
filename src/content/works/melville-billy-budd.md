synopsis: >-
  In 1797, the year of the great mutinies in the British navy, the handsome and innocent young sailor Billy Budd is pressed from a merchant ship onto the warship Bellipotent. The master-at-arms John Claggart conceives an unexplained hatred for him and accuses him of plotting mutiny before Captain Vere. Billy, who stammers when agitated, cannot answer and strikes Claggart dead with a single blow. Vere, convinced of Billy's moral innocence but bound by the Articles of War, convenes a drumhead court that condemns him, and Billy is hanged at dawn blessing the captain. Melville left the manuscript unfinished at his death.
