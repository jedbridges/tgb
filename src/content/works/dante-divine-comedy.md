  Lost in a dark wood in the middle of his life, Dante is led by the poet Virgil down through the nine circles of Hell, where the damned are punished in ways that fit their sins, then up the seven terraces of Mount Purgatory, where souls are cleansed of the same sins in hope. At the summit Beatrice, the woman he loved on earth, takes over and guides him through the heavenly spheres to a vision of God. The poem is written in a hundred cantos of terza rima and populates the afterlife with figures from history, myth and Dante's own Florence.
