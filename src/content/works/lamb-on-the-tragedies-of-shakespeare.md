synopsis: >-
  An essay first published in Leigh Hunt's Reflector, arguing that Shakespeare's great tragedies are better read than seen. Lamb contends that acting reduces Hamlet's inward thought to gesture, that Lear's storm and madness cannot be represented without becoming painful or absurd, and that stage Richards and Iagos make villainy attractive. The essay speaks for the Romantic view of Shakespeare as a poet of the mind, and was long quoted against the theatre.
