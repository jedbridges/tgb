synopsis: >-
  Livy's history of Rome from its founding in 753 BCE to his own day, written under Augustus in 142 books, of which 35 survive. The first five books tell the legends of Romulus and Remus, the rape of Lucretia and the expulsion of the kings, the founding of the republic, the struggle between patricians and plebeians, Coriolanus, Cincinnatus, the Twelve Tables, and the sack of Rome by the Gauls. Livy offers Roman history as a gallery of examples of virtue and its decline.
