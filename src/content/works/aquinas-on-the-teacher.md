synopsis: >-
  Question eleven of Aquinas' Disputed Questions on Truth, taking up Augustine's dialogue of the same name. Four articles ask whether one human being can teach another or only God can, whether a person can be called his own teacher, whether angels teach humans, and whether teaching belongs to the active or the contemplative life. Aquinas answers that a teacher does cause knowledge in a student, but only by presenting signs that help the learner's own natural reason move from what it already knows to what it does not.
