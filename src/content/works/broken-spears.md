synopsis: >-
  An anthology of Nahuatl accounts of the Spanish conquest, edited by the Mexican historian Miguel León-Portilla from sixteenth-century sources, chiefly the informants of Sahagún, translated into Spanish by Ángel María Garibay. It follows the omens before Cortés's arrival, Moctezuma's embassies and his capture, the massacre at the festival of Toxcatl, the Night of Sorrows, the siege and fall of Tenochtitlan in 1521, and the elegies the survivors sang. It gives the conquered a voice the Spanish chronicles lack.
