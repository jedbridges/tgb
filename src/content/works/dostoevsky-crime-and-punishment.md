synopsis: >-
  Raskolnikov, a poor former student in St Petersburg, murders an old pawnbroker and her sister with an axe, half to relieve his poverty and half to test his theory that extraordinary men may step over the law. The rest of the novel follows his fever, his cat-and-mouse with the detective Porfiry, his family's troubles and his friendship with Sonya, a young prostitute who reads him the raising of Lazarus. He confesses and is sent to Siberia, where the epilogue shows the beginning of a new life.
