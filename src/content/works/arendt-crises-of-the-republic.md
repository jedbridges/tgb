synopsis: >-
  Four essays written between 1969 and 1971 in response to the Vietnam War, the Pentagon Papers and the student movements. Lying in Politics examines how deception and image-making replaced facts in American war policy. Civil Disobedience argues that organised lawbreaking by minorities is a legitimate form of voluntary association in the American tradition. On Violence distinguishes power, which rests on the consent of a group, from violence, which is instrumental and destroys power. The last piece is an interview on politics and revolution.
