synopsis: >-
  The systematic statement of Reformed theology, first published as a short handbook in 1536 and expanded through five editions to four books. Book I treats the knowledge of God as creator, scripture and providence; Book II the fall, human depravity and Christ the redeemer; Book III faith, justification by grace alone and the doctrine of predestination; Book IV the church, sacraments and civil government. The last chapter, on civil government, sets out the duties of magistrates and the limits of obedience.
