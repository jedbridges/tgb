synopsis: >-
  Argan is a healthy man convinced he is dying, surrounded by doctors and apothecaries who profit from his fear. He wants to marry his daughter Angélique to a young physician so that he can have medical care in the family, while his second wife waits for him to die and leave her everything. The servant Toinette and Argan's brother Béralde expose the wife by having Argan play dead, and the play ends with a burlesque ceremony in which Argan is made a doctor himself. Molière, playing Argan, collapsed during the fourth performance and died the same night.
