synopsis: >-
  An essay based on a lecture Thoreau gave after spending a night in the Concord jail in 1846 for refusing to pay a poll tax that supported slavery and the Mexican War. He argues that the best government is the one that governs least, that a person's first obligation is to do what he thinks right rather than what the majority decides, and that when a government commits injustice a citizen should withdraw his support from it, even at the cost of prison. Published as Resistance to Civil Government in 1849, it was retitled after his death and later influenced Gandhi and Martin Luther King.
