synopsis: >-
  A collection of about five hundred short sayings, conversations and anecdotes of Confucius, compiled by his disciples and their successors over the centuries after his death in 479 BCE. Arranged in twenty books with no system, they concern the cultivation of the gentleman, ritual propriety, filial piety, learning, humaneness, and the right way to serve a ruler. The Harvard Classics prints Lionel Giles's selection under the title The Sayings of Confucius.
