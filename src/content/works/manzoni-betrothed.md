synopsis: >-
  A historical novel set in Lombardy under Spanish rule in 1628-1630. Renzo and Lucia, two silk workers in a village on Lake Como, are about to marry when the local lord Don Rodrigo, who has a bet on seducing Lucia, frightens the parish priest Don Abbondio out of performing the wedding. The couple flee separately; Renzo is caught up in the Milan bread riots, and Lucia is kidnapped for Don Rodrigo by a great criminal called the Unnamed, whose sudden conversion frees her. Famine, the passage of foreign armies and the plague of 1630 follow, and the lovers are reunited in the plague hospital. The book is the national novel of Italy.
