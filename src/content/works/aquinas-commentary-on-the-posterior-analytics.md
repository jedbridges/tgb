synopsis: >-
  A line-by-line exposition of Aristotle's treatise on demonstrative knowledge, written near the end of Aquinas' life. The Proemium, the part most often assigned, divides logic into its branches and locates the Posterior Analytics as the science of the demonstration that produces certain knowledge from its causes. The commentary then follows Aristotle through what a demonstration is, what its premises must be, how definitions relate to causes, and how first principles are grasped.
