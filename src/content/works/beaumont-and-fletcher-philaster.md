  A tragicomedy that made the collaboration of Beaumont and Fletcher famous. Philaster, rightful heir to the throne of Sicily, loves the princess Arethusa, whose father the usurping king plans to marry her to the Spanish prince Pharamond. Philaster gives her his page Bellario as a go-between; a false accusation that Arethusa and the page are lovers drives him to wound her in the forest. A popular rising restores him, and Bellario proves to be a girl in disguise, in love with Philaster all along.
