synopsis: >-
  Malory's compilation of the Arthurian legend, finished in prison in 1469 or 1470 and printed by Caxton in 1485 as twenty-one books. It follows Arthur from his birth and the sword in the stone through the founding of the Round Table, the adventures of Lancelot, Gareth and Tristram, the quest of the Holy Grail achieved by Galahad, the adultery of Lancelot and Guinevere, the war it causes, Mordred's treason and the last battle in which Arthur is carried away to Avalon. It is the source of most later English versions of the story.
