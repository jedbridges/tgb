synopsis: >-
  The diary of Antoine Roquentin, a solitary historian living in the seaside town of Bouville while he researches a biography of an obscure eighteenth-century marquis. Ordinary objects begin to unsettle him: a pebble on the beach, a glass of beer, his own hand. He gives up the biography, quarrels with a self-taught humanist who has read the library in alphabetical order, and in a park before a chestnut tree root he sees existence itself, gratuitous and without justification. At the end a jazz record suggests the only way out he can imagine, which is to make something.
