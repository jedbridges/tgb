synopsis: >-
  Fabrizio del Dongo, a young Lombard aristocrat, runs off to fight for Napoleon and wanders lost across the field of Waterloo without understanding what he sees. Back in Italy his aunt Gina, the Duchess Sanseverina, and her lover Count Mosca, the prime minister of the little court of Parma, manoeuvre to advance him in the Church. Fabrizio kills a man in a brawl, is imprisoned in the Farnese tower and there falls in love with Clelia, the governor's daughter. Stendhal dictated the novel in seven weeks.
