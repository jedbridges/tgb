synopsis: >-
  Epicurus' short summary of his ethics, preserved by Diogenes Laertius. It tells the young Menoeceus that philosophy is never too early or too late, that the gods exist but do not concern themselves with us, that death is nothing to us because where death is we are not, and that pleasure, understood as freedom from bodily pain and mental disturbance, is the goal of life. Prudence, not indulgence, is the chief virtue, and the wise man laughs at fate.
