synopsis: >-
  Rome's national epic, in twelve books, left unfinished at Virgil's death. Aeneas escapes burning Troy carrying his father and leading his son, and is driven by storm to Carthage, where he tells Queen Dido his story and becomes her lover before the gods order him on. She kills herself as he sails. After visiting his dead father in the underworld and seeing Rome's future, he lands in Italy, where a war with the Latins ends with him killing their champion Turnus in anger.
