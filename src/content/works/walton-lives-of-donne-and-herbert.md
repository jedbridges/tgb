synopsis: >-
  Two short biographies by the author of The Compleat Angler, who knew Donne and had friends who knew Herbert. The life of Donne, first printed with his sermons in 1640, follows him from his Catholic upbringing through his secret marriage and years of poverty to his ordination, his deanship of St Paul's, and his death, for which he posed in his shroud. The life of Herbert tells of a courtier who gave up preferment to become the rector of Bemerton and died at forty. Both are affectionate portraits of holy dying.
