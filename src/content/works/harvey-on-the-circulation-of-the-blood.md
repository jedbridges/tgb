synopsis: >-
  Two open letters, published in Cambridge in 1649, addressed to the Paris anatomist Jean Riolan, who had accepted a limited circulation but denied that it ran through the whole body. Harvey answers his objections one by one, restates the experiments of 1628, adds new ones on the pulse, the movement of blood in veins and the effect of cutting arteries, and replies to the wider criticism of the previous twenty years. The second letter also defends observation and experiment against argument from ancient authority.
