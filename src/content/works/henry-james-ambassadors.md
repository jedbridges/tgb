synopsis: >-
  Lambert Strether, a widower of fifty-five from Woollett, Massachusetts, is sent to Paris by his patron Mrs Newsome to bring home her son Chad, who is thought to be in the hands of a bad woman, and to prepare for marrying her. He finds Chad improved and the woman, Madame de Vionnet, a countess of great charm, and comes to believe their attachment is virtuous. Under the spell of Paris he tells a young man to live all he can. A second set of ambassadors arrives from Woollett; a chance meeting on a river reveals the truth of the affair; Strether, having lost his future in Woollett, refuses to gain anything for himself and goes home.
