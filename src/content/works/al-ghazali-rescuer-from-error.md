synopsis: >-
  A short spiritual autobiography written near the end of al-Ghazali's life. He describes how a crisis of doubt about the senses and reason led him to examine the four groups who claimed truth in his day, the theologians, the philosophers, the Ismaili followers of an infallible imam, and the Sufis. He finds the first three wanting and concludes that only the Sufi path of direct experience, not argument, gives certainty. He recounts leaving his professorship in Baghdad in 1095 for ten years of retreat and his reluctant return to teaching.
