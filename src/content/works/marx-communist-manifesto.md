synopsis: >-
  A pamphlet written by Marx with Friedrich Engels for the Communist League and published in London in February 1848, weeks before the revolutions of that year. Its first section argues that all history is the history of class struggle and that the bourgeoisie, having transformed the world, has created in the proletariat the class that will overthrow it. The second sets out the communists' programme, including the abolition of private property in the means of production; the third criticises rival socialisms; the fourth calls on the workers of all countries to unite.
