synopsis: >-
  A one-act prose comedy written in reply to the attacks on The School for Wives. In a Paris salon a prude, a fop, a pedantic poet and their more sensible hosts argue about whether the play is indecent, badly built and beneath the rules of art. Molière's spokesmen answer that the point of comedy is to please, that the rules are only common sense, and that the audience in the pit is a better judge than the critics. The play is the closest Molière came to writing a theory of his own comedy.
