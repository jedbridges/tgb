synopsis: >-
  Franklin's unfinished account of his life, begun as a letter to his son in 1771 and continued in three later instalments, ending in 1757. He describes his Boston boyhood, his apprenticeship in his brother's print shop, his flight to Philadelphia, his years in London, his rise as a printer and newspaper owner, and his civic projects, the library, the fire company, the academy and the militia. The most famous section lays out his plan for arriving at moral perfection by keeping a chart of thirteen virtues.
