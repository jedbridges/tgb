synopsis: >-
  Published under the pseudonym Johannes de Silentio, the book meditates on Genesis 22, in which God commands Abraham to sacrifice his son Isaac. After four retellings of the story and a tribute to Abraham, three problems ask whether there can be a teleological suspension of the ethical, whether there is an absolute duty to God, and whether Abraham was right to say nothing to Sarah, Eliezer and Isaac. Johannes contrasts the knight of infinite resignation, who gives up the finite, with the knight of faith, who gives it up and receives it back by virtue of the absurd, and confesses that he cannot understand Abraham.
