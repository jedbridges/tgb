synopsis: >-
  The first work of history in the Western tradition, in nine books, on the wars between Greece and Persia. Herodotus begins with the rise of Croesus and the Persian empire under Cyrus, then detours through Egypt, Babylon and Scythia, describing customs, monuments and marvels, before narrating Darius' failed expedition at Marathon and Xerxes' great invasion, the battles of Thermopylae, Salamis and Plataea, and the Greek victory. Throughout he records what he was told, weighs the versions, and returns to the theme that great prosperity invites a fall.
