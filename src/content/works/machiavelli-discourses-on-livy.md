synopsis: >-
  Machiavelli's longer and more republican book, a commentary on Livy's history of early Rome in three books of short chapters. Book I treats Rome's internal constitution and argues that the conflict between nobles and people was the source of its liberty; Book II covers its wars and expansion; Book III its great men and the need for a republic to return periodically to its founding principles. Religion, conspiracies, corruption and the ingratitude of peoples are recurring subjects.
