synopsis: >-
  An Old Irish prose tale from the Ulster cycle's neighbouring tradition of kings. Conaire Mór, son of a bird-man and a foundling girl, is made high king of Ireland under a set of prohibitions he must never break. Forced by circumstance to violate them one after another, he takes shelter in the hostel of Dá Derga, where his foster-brothers, turned raiders, and the British pirate Ingcél surround him. A long sequence describes each company in the hostel before the assault; Conaire kills hundreds, dies of thirst, and his severed head thanks the warrior who brings it water.
