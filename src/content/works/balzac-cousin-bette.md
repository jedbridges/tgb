synopsis: >-
  Lisbeth Fischer, a poor and plain spinster kept as a dependent by her rich relations the Hulots, nurses a lifelong envy of her beautiful cousin Adeline. When Adeline's daughter takes away the young Polish sculptor Bette has been supporting, Bette allies with the courtesan Valérie Marneffe to ruin the family. Baron Hulot, Adeline's husband, is a compulsive womaniser whose affairs and embezzlements do most of the work for her. Set in Paris under Louis-Philippe, it is one of the last and darkest novels of Balzac's Human Comedy.
