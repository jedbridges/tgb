synopsis: >-
  Mill argues that the only purpose for which power may rightly be used against an adult against his will is to prevent harm to others. He defends complete freedom of thought and discussion on the grounds that a silenced opinion may be true, may be partly true, or may keep a true opinion from hardening into dead dogma. He then defends individuality and experiments in living against the pressure of custom and public opinion, and works out where the principle stops: no liberty to harm, and no liberty for children.
