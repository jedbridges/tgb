synopsis: >-
  Montesquieu's treatise, twenty years in the making, examines the laws of many nations to find the relations that explain them. He classifies governments as republics, monarchies and despotisms, each with its animating principle of virtue, honour or fear. He describes the English constitution as a separation of legislative, executive and judicial powers that secures liberty, argues that climate and terrain shape a people's laws and character, and treats commerce, money, population, religion and the history of feudal law in France. The book was placed on the Index and shaped the American founders.
