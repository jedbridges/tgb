  Questions five and six of Aquinas' early commentary on Boethius' short treatise on the Trinity, published on their own under this title. Question five asks how the theoretical sciences are divided into natural philosophy, mathematics and metaphysics, and by what kind of abstraction each considers its object. Question six asks what method suits each science, whether reason or intellect leads, and whether the human mind can see the divine form in this life. The text is a compact statement of how Aquinas thinks knowledge is organised.
