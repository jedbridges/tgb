synopsis: >-
  Joyce's first novel follows Stephen Dedalus from infancy to his early twenties, its style growing with him from a child's babble to the diary of a would-be artist. Stephen is bullied at Clongowes, watches his family argue over Parnell at Christmas dinner, sinks into adolescent sin, is terrified into piety by a retreat sermon on hell, and is invited to become a Jesuit. On the strand he sees a girl wading and chooses art instead. At university he sets out his theory of beauty and resolves to leave Ireland, refusing to serve family, nation or church.
