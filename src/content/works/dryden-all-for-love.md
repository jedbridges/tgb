synopsis: >-
  Dryden's blank verse tragedy of Antony and Cleopatra, written in deliberate imitation of Shakespeare but obeying the unities. The action is confined to Alexandria on the last day of Antony's life. His general Ventidius, his friend Dolabella and his wife Octavia try in turn to draw him from Cleopatra back to Rome and honour; the eunuch Alexas's false report of her death brings on his suicide, and she follows him. Dryden's preface defends the play against French rules and English critics.
