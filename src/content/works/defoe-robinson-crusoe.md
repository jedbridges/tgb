synopsis: >-
  Crusoe goes to sea against his father's advice, is enslaved by corsairs, escapes, prospers as a Brazilian planter, and is shipwrecked alone on an island near the mouth of the Orinoco. He spends twenty-eight years there, and the book's substance is his inventory: what he salvaged, what he built, how long the fence took, how the barley grew, the pots that cracked. A fever and a Bible bring about his conversion. A footprint ends his solitude, and the arrival of cannibals gives him a servant, Friday, whom he teaches English and Christianity before a mutinous crew offers him a way home.
