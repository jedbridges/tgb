synopsis: >-
  Phaedo recounts Socrates' last day in prison, spent with his friends discussing whether the soul survives death. Socrates offers a series of arguments for immortality, including the theory of recollection and the claim that the soul is akin to the unchanging Forms, and answers objections from Simmias and Cebes. He closes with a myth of the afterlife, bathes, drinks the hemlock, and dies while his friends weep.
