synopsis: >-
  A poem in fifteen books and some 250 stories of transformation, running from the creation of the world out of chaos to the deification of Julius Caesar. Gods pursue nymphs who become trees and rivers, Narcissus falls in love with his reflection, Daedalus builds wings, Orpheus loses Eurydice and is torn apart by maenads, Pygmalion's statue comes to life, Iphis is changed from a girl to a boy to marry Ianthe. Stories nest inside stories, moving through Greek myth to the Trojan War and the founding of Rome, and the poem ends with Pythagoras on universal change and Ovid's claim that his work will outlive him.
