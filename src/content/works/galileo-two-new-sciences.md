synopsis: >-
  Galileo's last book, written under house arrest and printed in Leiden. Over four days Salviati, Sagredo and Simplicio discuss two new sciences, the strength of materials and the motion of bodies. The first two days treat cohesion, the breaking of beams, the vacuum and the infinite; the third and fourth give the mathematical laws of uniformly accelerated motion and projectiles, showing that falling bodies gain speed in proportion to time and that a projectile traces a parabola.
