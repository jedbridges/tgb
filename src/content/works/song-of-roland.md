synopsis: >-
  The oldest surviving French epic retells, as legend, the ambush of Charlemagne's rearguard at Roncevaux in 778. Roland's stepfather Ganelon, insulted by Roland, betrays the rearguard to the Saracen king Marsile. Roland refuses out of pride to blow his horn for help until his twelve peers and twenty thousand men are dying; he sounds it too late, dies facing Spain, and is received by angels. Charlemagne returns to avenge him in a great battle, and Ganelon is tried and torn apart by horses.
