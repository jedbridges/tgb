synopsis: >-
  An account of the war between Athens and Sparta from 431 to 411 BCE, where the text breaks off, written by an Athenian general exiled after losing Amphipolis. Thucydides explains the war's causes as the growth of Athenian power and Spartan fear, and narrates the plague, Pericles' funeral oration, the revolt of Mytilene, the civil war in Corcyra, the Melian dialogue and the disastrous Sicilian expedition. He set out, he says, to write a possession for all time rather than a prize composition.
