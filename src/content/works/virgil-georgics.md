synopsis: >-
  A didactic poem in four books on farming, addressed to Maecenas and finished as Octavian was consolidating power. Book one treats crops, weather and the signs of civil war; book two trees and vines, with the praise of Italy; book three cattle and horses, ending in a plague; book four bees and their society, closing with the story of the beekeeper Aristaeus and the descent of Orpheus to recover Eurydice. The poem's subject is labour, and the cost of the order it wins from nature.
