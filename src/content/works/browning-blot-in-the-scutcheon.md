synopsis: >-
  A verse tragedy in three acts, first staged by Macready at Drury Lane in 1843. Lord Tresham, proud of his family's unstained honour, agrees to give his fourteen-year-old sister Mildred to the young Earl Mertoun, not knowing that the two are already secret lovers. A servant reveals that a man has been climbing to Mildred's window at night. Tresham confronts and kills the disguised Mertoun before learning his identity, Mildred dies of grief, and Tresham poisons himself. The play takes place in the eighteenth century and turns on the code of honour it depicts.
