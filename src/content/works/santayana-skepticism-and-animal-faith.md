synopsis: >-
  The introduction to Santayana's mature system. He pushes doubt further than Descartes, to a solipsism of the present moment in which only essences, pure qualities and forms, are given and nothing can be known to exist. Since this is unlivable, he argues, all belief in existence rests on animal faith, the trust in a surrounding world that every creature acts on before it reasons. From this he recovers matter, the self, memory and science as objects of faith, and defines the realms of essence, matter, truth and spirit.
