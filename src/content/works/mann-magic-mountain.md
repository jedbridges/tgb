synopsis: >-
  Hans Castorp, a young engineer from Hamburg, goes to visit his cousin at a tuberculosis sanatorium in Davos for three weeks and stays seven years. Diagnosed with a spot on his lung, he settles into the suspended routine of rest cures and meals, falls in love with the Russian patient Clawdia Chauchat, and becomes the pupil of two quarrelling mentors, the humanist Settembrini and the Jesuit-trained radical Naphta, who fight over his soul and finally over a duel. Time dissolves, he has a vision in the snow, and the outbreak of the First World War sends him down the mountain to the battlefield.
