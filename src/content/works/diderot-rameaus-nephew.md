synopsis: >-
  A dialogue in a Paris café between Moi, a philosopher, and Lui, the nephew of the composer Rameau, a failed musician, parasite and mimic who lives by flattering the rich. Lui defends with brilliance and candour a life of sponging, lying and self-interest, argues that genius and virtue rarely go together, acts out whole operas and dinner parties, and unsettles the philosopher's confidence in the reward of virtue without quite defeating it. Diderot never published it; the manuscript reached Goethe, who translated it into German in 1805, and the French text appeared later.
