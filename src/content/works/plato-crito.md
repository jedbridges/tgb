synopsis: >-
  Socrates' old friend Crito visits him in prison before dawn and urges him to escape, saying the plan is ready and that staying will shame his friends and abandon his children. Socrates replies that the only question is whether escaping would be just. Speaking in the voice of the Laws of Athens, he argues that he has agreed to live under them all his life and that returning wrong for wrong is never right, so he will stay and die.
