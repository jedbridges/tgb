synopsis: >-
  Shipwrecked on the coast of Illyria and believing her twin brother Sebastian drowned, Viola disguises herself as a boy, Cesario, and enters the service of Duke Orsino, whom she comes to love. Orsino sends her to woo the countess Olivia on his behalf, and Olivia falls for the messenger. Below stairs, Olivia's drunken uncle Sir Toby and her maid trick the puritanical steward Malvolio into believing his mistress loves him. Sebastian's arrival untangles the lovers; Malvolio is left humiliated.
