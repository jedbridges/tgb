synopsis: >-
  Congreve's last comedy, first performed in March 1700 and coolly received. Mirabell wants to marry Millamant, but her aunt Lady Wishfort, whom he once pretended to court, controls half her fortune and hates him. His scheme to trap Lady Wishfort into consent with a fake suitor is exposed by Mrs Marwood, who wants Mirabell herself, and Fainall, Lady Wishfort's son-in-law, tries to blackmail the family with his wife's past. A deed of trust that Mirabell holds defeats Fainall. The proviso scene, in which Mirabell and Millamant set out the conditions on which they will marry, is the most famous in Restoration comedy.
