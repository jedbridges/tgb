synopsis: >-
  Locke's account of where legitimate government comes from. Human beings in the state of nature are free and equal under a law of nature that forbids harming another's life, liberty or property; property arises when labour is mixed with what nature provides. Because enforcing that law by oneself is unreliable, people consent to form a political society and entrust legislative power to a government limited to protecting their rights. When rulers break that trust the people may dissolve the government and resist. Published anonymously after the Glorious Revolution.
