synopsis: >-
  The longest of the Icelandic family sagas, written in prose around 1280 about events of about 960 to 1020. Its first half follows the friendship of the wise lawyer Njal and the warrior Gunnar of Hlidarendi, whose wife Hallgerd's feud with Njal's wife Bergthora runs through a chain of killings and settlements until Gunnar is outlawed and killed at his home. In the second half Njal's sons kill their foster-brother Hoskuld; the resulting suit at the Althing collapses into violence, Njal and his family are burned alive in their house by Flosi, and Kari pursues the burners across Iceland and abroad until the feud ends in reconciliation.
