synopsis: >-
  After the fall of Troy, Hector's widow Andromache is the captive of Pyrrhus, son of Achilles, who loves her and will spare her son Astyanax only if she marries him. Pyrrhus is betrothed to Hermione, who loves him; the Greek envoy Orestes loves Hermione and has come to demand the child's death. Andromache agrees to marry Pyrrhus intending to kill herself afterwards. Hermione orders Orestes to kill Pyrrhus, then turns on him when he does, and Orestes goes mad.
