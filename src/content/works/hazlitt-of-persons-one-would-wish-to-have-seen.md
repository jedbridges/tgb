synopsis: >-
  An essay recalling an evening at Charles Lamb's some twenty years earlier, when the company played the game of naming which dead persons they would most like to have met. Lamb, the essay's real subject, rejects the obvious choices, argues for Sir Thomas Browne and Fulke Greville, and at the end names Christ, before which the room falls silent. Along the way the talk takes in Chaucer, Shakespeare, Newton, Locke, Pope, Dr Johnson, Rousseau and the conversation of geniuses generally.
