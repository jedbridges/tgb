synopsis: >-
  A vast verse drama, never staged in Hugo's lifetime, about Oliver Cromwell in 1657 hesitating whether to accept the crown, surrounded by royalist and Puritan plotters. It is remembered for its Preface, the manifesto of French Romanticism. There Hugo divides history into primitive, ancient and modern ages with lyric, epic and drama as their forms, argues that modern art must mix the sublime and the grotesque as Shakespeare did, and rejects the classical unities of time and place.
