synopsis: >-
  The private notebooks of a Roman emperor, written in Greek during his campaigns on the Danube and never intended for publication. Book I thanks the people who shaped him. The other eleven books are short entries in which Marcus reminds himself of Stoic principles: that only what is in our power matters, that everything is fleeting and governed by reason, that other people's faults come from ignorance, and that death is natural and near. The tone is stern, weary and consoling.
