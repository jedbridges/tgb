synopsis: >-
  Notes dictated to a small group of students at Cambridge in 1933 and 1934, circulated in blue covers and published after Wittgenstein's death. They open with the question what is the meaning of a word, and refuse to answer it by pointing to a mental object. Instead Wittgenstein introduces the ideas that would carry the later philosophy: language games, family resemblance rather than shared essence, the craving for generality as a philosophical disease, and the argument that talking about my pain and your pain does not describe two instances of one private thing. It is the shortest way into the late Wittgenstein.
