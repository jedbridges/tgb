synopsis: >-
  Epictetus wrote nothing. These are notes taken by his student Arrian at the school in Nicopolis, four books of the eight surviving, and they read like transcripts of a teacher arguing with young men who have come for a polish rather than a cure. The subject throughout is the distinction between what is in our power and what is not, and the training needed to live by it. Epictetus was born a slave and speaks constantly of freedom, tells students their philosophy is worthless if it does not change how they behave when a lamp is stolen or a tyrant threatens, and is frequently funny.
