synopsis: >-
  Proclaimed at Gonaïves on 1 January 1804 after the defeat of Napoleon's army, and drafted for Dessalines by his secretary Louis Boisrond-Tonnerre. It renounces France forever, names the new nation Haiti after the island's indigenous name, recalls the cruelties of slavery and the war, and swears the generals and people to die rather than live again under French rule. Its language is far angrier than the American declaration it is often compared with.
