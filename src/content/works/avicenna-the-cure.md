synopsis: >-
  Avicenna's encyclopedia of philosophy, the largest work of its kind by a single author, in four parts covering logic, natural philosophy, mathematics and metaphysics. The section on the soul, part of the natural philosophy, argues for the soul's immateriality with the thought experiment of the flying man, who, suspended in air with no sensation, would still know that he exists. The metaphysics distinguishes essence from existence and proves a Necessary Existent. The book reached Latin Europe in the twelfth century and shaped Aquinas and the scholastics.
