synopsis: >-
  A short paper written in 1915 and published in 1917 that compares ordinary grief with pathological depression. In mourning the world has become poor and empty; in melancholia it is the ego itself. Freud proposes that the melancholic has unconsciously identified with a lost object and turned the reproaches meant for it against himself, so that the shadow of the object falls upon the ego. The paper introduced the idea of identification that reshaped his later theory.
