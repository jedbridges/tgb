synopsis: >-
  In a framing scene a drunken tinker is tricked into thinking he is a lord and shown a play. In it, the wealthy Baptista of Padua will not let his popular daughter Bianca marry until her sharp-tongued elder sister Katherina has a husband. The fortune-hunter Petruchio takes the job, marries Kate against her will and subjects her to a regime of hunger, sleeplessness and contradiction until she submits. Bianca's suitors compete by disguise. At the closing feast Kate delivers a long speech on wifely obedience.
