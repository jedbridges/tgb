  Twenty-five short letters written after Voltaire's exile in England from 1726 to 1729, describing a country he holds up as a mirror to France. He reports on the Quakers, the Church of England, Parliament, trade and the Royal Exchange, inoculation against smallpox, Bacon, Locke and Newton, and English tragedy and comedy. The praise of English religious toleration, constitutional government and experimental science was understood as an attack on France; the book was condemned and burned in Paris in 1734.
