synopsis: >-
  Medea, a foreign princess and sorceress who betrayed her family to help Jason win the Golden Fleece, has been abandoned by him in Corinth so that he can marry the king's daughter. Sentenced to exile, she bargains for a single day, secures a refuge in Athens from the visiting king Aegeus, and uses the day to kill Jason's bride and her father with a poisoned robe. Then, to complete his ruin, she kills their two sons and escapes in the chariot of the sun god, leaving Jason with nothing.
