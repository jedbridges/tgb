synopsis: >-
  Written from Macedonia after a painful visit to Corinth and a severe letter now lost, this is the most personal of Paul's letters. In its first part he explains his change of travel plans, describes his ministry as a treasure in clay jars and a ministry of reconciliation, and rejoices at the Corinthians' repentance. Chapters 8 and 9 appeal for the collection for the poor in Jerusalem. The last four chapters turn sharply to attack rival super-apostles, and Paul boasts, as a fool, of his sufferings, his vision of the third heaven and the thorn in his flesh.
