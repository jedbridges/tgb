synopsis: >-
  The essay in which Freud sets out the structural model of the mind. The id is the reservoir of the drives, wholly unconscious; the ego grows out of it under pressure from the external world and mediates between the two; the superego, heir to the Oedipus complex, is the internalised parental authority that judges the ego. Freud shows that much of the ego and the superego are themselves unconscious, and ends on the ego's dependence on its three harsh masters.
