synopsis: >-
  A Greek hostage in Rome set out to explain how Rome conquered nearly the whole known world in fifty-three years, from 220 to 167 BCE. Of forty books, the first five survive complete and the rest in extracts. They cover the Punic Wars, Hannibal's crossing of the Alps and Cannae, the wars in Greece and Macedon, and the sack of Carthage. Book VI, the most read, analyses the Roman constitution as a mixture of monarchy, aristocracy and democracy and describes the cycle through which constitutions decay.
