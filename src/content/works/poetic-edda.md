synopsis: >-
  A collection of Old Norse poems preserved mainly in the Icelandic Codex Regius, written around 1270 but containing verse composed centuries earlier. The mythological poems include the Völuspá, in which a seeress recounts the creation of the world and foretells its destruction at Ragnarök; the Hávamál, sayings attributed to Odin; and comic and grim tales of Thor, Loki and the giants. The heroic poems tell the story of Sigurd, the dragon Fafnir, Brynhild and Gudrun, the same legend told in prose in the Völsunga saga and in German in the Nibelungenlied.
