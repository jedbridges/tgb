  A narrative poem in dactylic hexameter, the most popular American poem of its century. In 1755 the British expel the French settlers of Acadia in Nova Scotia; Evangeline is separated on her wedding day from her betrothed Gabriel. She searches for him for years across Louisiana, the western prairies and the Ozarks, always just missing him, and finds him at last as an old woman nursing the poor in a Philadelphia almshouse, where he dies in her arms. The forest primeval opening became a schoolroom staple.
