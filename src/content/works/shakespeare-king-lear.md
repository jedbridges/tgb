synopsis: >-
  The old king of Britain divides his kingdom between the two daughters who flatter him and disinherits Cordelia, the one who will not. Goneril and Regan strip him of his retinue and shut him out in a storm, where he goes mad in the company of his Fool and a disguised Edgar. A parallel plot follows Gloucester, deceived by his bastard son Edmund and blinded for helping Lear. Cordelia returns with a French army, loses, and is hanged; Lear dies carrying her body.
