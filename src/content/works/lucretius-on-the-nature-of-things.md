synopsis: >-
  A Latin poem in six books, some 7,400 hexameter lines, expounding the philosophy of Epicurus to a Roman patron named Memmius. Books 1 and 2 argue that everything is made of atoms moving in a void, with an unpredictable swerve that allows free will. Book 3 argues that the soul is material and dies with the body, so that death is nothing to us. Book 4 treats the senses, sleep and sexual passion; Book 5 the origin of the world and of civilisation; Book 6 weather, earthquakes and disease, ending with the plague at Athens.
