synopsis: >-
  A short dialogue set in 129 BCE, a few days after the death of Scipio Aemilianus, in which his friend Laelius explains friendship to his sons-in-law. Laelius argues that true friendship is possible only between good men, that it arises from virtue rather than need, and that it must never be used to ask or grant anything dishonourable. He discusses how friends are chosen, how friendships end, the limits of loyalty in politics, and whether it is wise to love in order to avoid grief, and ends by ranking friendship after virtue as the best thing in life.
