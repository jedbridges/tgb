synopsis: >-
  Written in Paris during the English Civil War, Leviathan builds a science of politics from a mechanical account of human nature. Part one describes sensation, reason and the passions, and argues that without a common power life is a war of every man against every man, solitary, poor, nasty, brutish and short. Part two shows how people escape it by covenanting to give up their right to everything to a sovereign whose power must be absolute and undivided. Parts three and four subject Scripture and the churches to the same sovereign and attack the Kingdom of Darkness.
