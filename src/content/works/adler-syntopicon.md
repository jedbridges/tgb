synopsis: >-
  The first two volumes of Great Books of the Western World are not a work of literature but a reference tool. Adler and a staff of indexers spent eight years reading the set and organising it under 102 Great Ideas, from Angel to World. For each idea there is an introductory essay by Adler, an outline of topics, references to passages in every author in the set, cross-references and a bibliography. An appendix on the principles of the Syntopicon explains the method, and Adler's essay The Great Conversation, in Volume 1 of the 1952 edition, states the case for the whole enterprise.
