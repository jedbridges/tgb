synopsis: >-
  Tocqueville's report on the United States after a nine month journey in 1831 and 1832, written as an account of what the equality of conditions does to a people. The first volume, of 1835, describes the physical setting, the townships and states, the federal constitution, the press, political associations, juries, the power of the majority and the position of the three races on the continent. The second volume, of 1840, turns from institutions to habits of mind and heart, treating American philosophy, religion, the arts, ambition, individualism, the family, the relations of men and women, honour and manners, and ends by warning of a new kind of mild administrative despotism.
