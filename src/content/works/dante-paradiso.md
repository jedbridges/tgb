synopsis: >-
  The third canticle of the Divine Comedy. Guided by Beatrice, Dante rises through the spheres of the Moon, Mercury, Venus, the Sun, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn and the fixed stars, meeting souls arranged by the form their blessedness took, among them Justinian, Aquinas, his ancestor Cacciaguida, who foretells his exile, and the apostles who examine him on faith, hope and love. In the Empyrean, Bernard of Clairvaux takes over and prays for Dante to see God directly. The poem ends with a vision of the Trinity and the love that moves the sun and the other stars.
