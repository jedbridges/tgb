synopsis: >-
  A comic epic in ottava rima, left unfinished at Byron's death after sixteen cantos. Its Juan is not the seducer of legend but a good-natured young Spaniard whom women seduce. Sent abroad after an affair, he is shipwrecked, rescued by the pirate's daughter Haidée, sold into a Turkish harem, present at the Russian siege of Ismail, taken up by Catherine the Great and dispatched to England, where the poem turns to satire of society and its digressive narrator talks about everything from cannibalism to poetic rivals.
