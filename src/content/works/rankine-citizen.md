synopsis: >-
  A book-length poem in prose paragraphs, lyric fragments, essays and reproduced artworks about what racism does to a person day by day. The opening sections accumulate small encounters in the second person, a colleague's remark, a stranger taking a seat elsewhere, a friend using the wrong name, and track the exhaustion and self-division they leave. Later sections turn outward to public figures and events: Serena Williams and the officials who called her lines, Zinedine Zidane's headbutt, Hurricane Katrina, Trayvon Martin and a list of the dead that the printings keep extending.
