synopsis: >-
  Tolstoy's late treatise attacking the idea that art is about beauty. He surveys two centuries of aesthetic theory, rejects it, and defines art as the transmission of feeling from one person to others by means of signs. Good art is sincere, clear and infectious; the best art conveys religious feeling and unites people. On this test he condemns most of the art of the upper classes, including Wagner, Baudelaire and his own novels, and praises folk art and the parables of the Gospels.
