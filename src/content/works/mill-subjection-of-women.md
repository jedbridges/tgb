synopsis: >-
  Mill argues that the legal subordination of women to men is a survival of the law of force, the last relic of a world in which the strong simply ruled the weak, and that it should be replaced by perfect equality before the law. He attacks the claim that women's present character shows their nature, since that character has been produced by the very conditions under discussion, and insists that no one can know what women can do until the artificial barriers come down. He describes marriage as it then stood as a form of legal despotism, and argues that equality would benefit men as much as women.
