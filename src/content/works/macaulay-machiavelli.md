synopsis: >-
  A review essay published in the Edinburgh Review in 1827 on a new edition of Machiavelli. Macaulay sets out to explain how a man of evident patriotism and integrity could have written The Prince. His answer is that Italian society in the Renaissance valued cunning over courage, as the northern nations valued courage over cunning, so that Machiavelli's morality was that of his time rather than a peculiar wickedness. The essay surveys Machiavelli's comedies, histories and political writings and closes with an appreciation of him as an Italian patriot.
