synopsis: >-
  A short Latin pamphlet, published in Venice in March 1610, reporting what Galileo saw through the telescope he had built the previous year. The moon has mountains and valleys like the earth; the Milky Way is made of countless stars; and four moons circle Jupiter, which Galileo named the Medicean stars after his patron. The book made him famous across Europe and gave the first observational evidence against a perfect, unchanging heaven.
