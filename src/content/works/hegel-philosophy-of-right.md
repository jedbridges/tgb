synopsis: >-
  Hegel's political philosophy, published as a textbook for his Berlin lectures. It moves from abstract right, the realm of persons, property and contract, through morality, the standpoint of the individual conscience, to ethical life, which unfolds in the family, civil society and the state. Hegel treats the modern market economy and its poverty, argues for a constitutional monarchy with estates and a professional civil service, and ends with world history as the court of judgement on states. The preface contains the claim that what is rational is actual and what is actual is rational.
