synopsis: >-
  A fiction in two books, written in Latin for humanist readers. In the first, More meets the traveller Raphael Hythloday in Antwerp and they debate whether a philosopher should advise kings, with Hythloday attacking enclosure and the hanging of thieves in England. In the second, Hythloday describes the island of Utopia, where property is held in common, everyone works six hours a day, gold is used for chamber pots, and religious toleration is law. Whether More endorses it or is joking has been argued ever since.
