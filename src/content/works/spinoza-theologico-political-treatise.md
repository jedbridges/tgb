synopsis: >-
  Published anonymously and quickly banned, this is Spinoza's argument that freedom of thought is compatible with piety and necessary to a stable state. He examines prophecy, the election of the Hebrews, divine law, miracles and the authorship of scripture, and concludes that the Bible is a historical document written for a particular people in a particular idiom, that it teaches obedience and justice rather than metaphysics, and that miracles cannot suspend the order of nature. The later chapters treat natural right, the transfer of power to the sovereign, the record of the Hebrew state, and why the sovereign should leave judgement and speech free.
