synopsis: >-
  A pamphlet published in September 1791, dedicated to Marie Antoinette, that rewrites the 1789 Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen article by article to include women. Woman is born free and equal to man in rights; she has the right to mount the scaffold and so must have the right to mount the rostrum. A preamble, seventeen articles and a postscript call on women to wake up, and a model marriage contract proposes an equal partnership of property. De Gouges was guillotined in 1793.
