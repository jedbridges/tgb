synopsis: >-
  Heidegger's inaugural lecture as professor at Freiburg in 1929. Beginning from the sciences, which deal with beings and nothing besides, he asks about that nothing. It cannot be reached by logic, but it is revealed in the mood of anxiety, in which beings as a whole slip away and we are held out into the nothing. This experience, he argues, is what makes it possible to ask about Being at all, and metaphysics is the questioning that goes beyond beings. The lecture ends by restating the question why there are beings rather than nothing.
