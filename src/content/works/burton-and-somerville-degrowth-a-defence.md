synopsis: >-
  An essay in New Left Review answering critics on the left who treat degrowth as a middle class enthusiasm that would condemn the poor to austerity. Burton and Somerville argue that the physical throughput of rich economies cannot keep expanding on a finite planet, that the evidence for decoupling growth from emissions and material use is weak, and that green growth therefore offers no way out. They distinguish degrowth from recession, sketch what a planned contraction of energy and material use would mean for work, provision and welfare, and argue that it is compatible with rising living standards for the poorest.
