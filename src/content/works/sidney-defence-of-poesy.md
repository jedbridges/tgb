synopsis: >-
  The first major work of literary criticism in English, written in answer to Puritan attacks on the stage and published after Sidney's death. Sidney argues that poetry is the oldest of learning and the best teacher of virtue, because it joins the philosopher's precept to the historian's example in a golden world of its own making. He answers the charges that poets lie and corrupt, then turns on the state of English writing, praising Chaucer and the old ballad of Chevy Chase and mocking the disorder of contemporary plays.
