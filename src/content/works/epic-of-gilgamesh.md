synopsis: >-
  Gilgamesh, king of Uruk, is two-thirds god and oppresses his people until the gods create the wild man Enkidu to match him. The two fight, become friends, and travel together to kill the monster Humbaba in the Cedar Forest and then the Bull of Heaven. The gods punish Enkidu with death, and Gilgamesh, terrified of dying, journeys to the ends of the earth to find Utnapishtim, the survivor of the flood, who tells him that immortality is not for men. He returns to Uruk and looks at its walls.
