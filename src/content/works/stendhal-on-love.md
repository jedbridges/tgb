  An analytical essay on love written after Stendhal's unrequited passion for Matilde Dembowski in Milan. He distinguishes passionate love, mannered love, physical love and vanity love, and describes the seven stages by which a person falls in love, the second of which he names crystallisation, the process by which the mind covers the beloved with perfections as a bare branch left in a salt mine comes out covered with crystals. Later chapters compare love in different countries, discuss the education of women and collect anecdotes and maxims.
