synopsis: >-
  A comedy first acted in 1610. While his master Lovewit has fled the plague, the servant Face sets up with the conman Subtle and the prostitute Dol Common in the empty London house, where they pose as an alchemist and his assistants. A stream of gulls arrives, the clerk Dapper, the tobacconist Drugger, the greedy Sir Epicure Mammon, two Puritan brethren and the angry boy Kastril, each promised what he wants. The plot keeps the unities of place and time to the hour and collapses when Lovewit returns.
