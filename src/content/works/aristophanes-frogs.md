synopsis: >-
  With Euripides and Sophocles both dead, the god Dionysus, patron of drama, decides Athens needs a good tragedian back and goes down to Hades to fetch Euripides. Disguised as Heracles and accompanied by his slave Xanthias, he rows across the underworld lake to a chorus of croaking frogs and survives a series of beatings and identity swaps. In Hades he finds Aeschylus and Euripides fighting over the throne of tragedy and is made judge. After a contest of quotations weighed on scales, he chooses Aeschylus, the poet who can save the city.
