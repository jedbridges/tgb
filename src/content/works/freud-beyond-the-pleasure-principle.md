synopsis: >-
  A short speculative essay in which Freud revises his theory of the mind. Observing that shell-shocked soldiers and a small child playing with a spool (the fort-da game) repeat painful experiences rather than avoid them, he posits a compulsion to repeat that lies beyond the pleasure principle. From it he derives the death drive, an urge in all living matter to return to an inanimate state, set against the life drives of Eros.
