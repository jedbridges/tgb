synopsis: >-
  Kepler's attempt to show that the universe is built on the same proportions as music. The first two books treat regular polygons and the tilings and solids they form; the third derives musical consonance from geometry; the fourth applies harmony to astrology and the soul. Book V, the one programs assign, finds harmonies in the speeds of the planets as seen from the sun, gives each a range of notes, and states in passing the third law of planetary motion that Newton would later need.
