synopsis: >-
  A novel of Puritan Boston in the 1640s. Hester Prynne, whose husband is presumed lost at sea, has borne a child and is condemned to wear a scarlet A for adultery. She refuses to name the father, the minister Arthur Dimmesdale, whose hidden guilt wastes him. Her husband arrives under the name Roger Chillingworth and devotes himself to tormenting the minister. After seven years Dimmesdale confesses on the scaffold and dies. The book opens with a long sketch of the Salem Custom-House.
