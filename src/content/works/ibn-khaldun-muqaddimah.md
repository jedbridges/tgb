synopsis: >-
  The introduction to Ibn Khaldun's universal history, written in 1377 while he was in retreat in what is now Algeria, and a book about how to think about history at all. He begins by exposing the errors and credulity of earlier historians and proposes instead a science of the underlying causes of social change. Its central concept is asabiyya, the group solidarity that binds kin in hard conditions; nomadic peoples have it and use it to conquer settled ones, then lose it over three or four generations as city life makes them soft, until a new group with asabiyya arrives. Later chapters treat dynasties, taxation, crafts, cities, trade and the sciences.
