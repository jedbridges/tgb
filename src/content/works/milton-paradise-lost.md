  An epic in twelve books of blank verse on the fall of man. Satan and his rebel angels, cast into Hell, resolve on revenge, and Satan flies through Chaos to the newly created Earth. Adam and Eve live in Eden until Satan, in the form of a serpent, tempts Eve to eat the forbidden fruit; Adam eats too rather than lose her. The Son of God judges them, the angel Michael shows Adam the future of the human race up to the Redemption, and the pair leave Paradise hand in hand.
