synopsis: >-
  A long essay, first published in a journal, that gives the most compact statement of Bergson's method. He distinguishes two ways of knowing a thing, moving round it through symbols and concepts, which is analysis, and entering into it, which is intuition. Metaphysics is the discipline that dispenses with symbols. The self in its duration is the one reality we know from within, and Bergson argues that the failures of both empiricism and rationalism come from trying to rebuild it out of fixed concepts.
