synopsis: >-
  A two-part verse drama on the legend of the scholar who bargains with the devil. In Part One, Faust, despairing of book learning, wagers with Mephistopheles that no moment will ever satisfy him; restored to youth, he seduces the innocent Gretchen, whose ruin ends with her in prison awaiting execution and her soul saved. Part Two moves through allegory and classical myth, from the emperor's court to Faust's union with Helen of Troy and his final project of reclaiming land from the sea, before angels carry his soul upward.
