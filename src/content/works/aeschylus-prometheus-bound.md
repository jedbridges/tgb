synopsis: >-
  The Titan Prometheus, who stole fire from the gods and gave it to mankind along with every art and science, is chained to a rock at the edge of the world on the orders of Zeus. Visited by the sea nymphs, by Ocean, and by Io, a girl turned into a heifer and driven across the earth by Hera's gadfly, he refuses to submit and prophesies that Zeus will one day be overthrown by a son of his own unless Prometheus reveals the secret. Hermes demands the secret, Prometheus refuses, and Zeus hurls him into the abyss. The play's authorship has been questioned since the nineteenth century.
