  Feuerbach's argument that theology is anthropology in disguise. Human beings project their own species-essence, reason, will and love, onto an imagined being outside themselves and then worship it; God's attributes are human attributes freed from limits. Part I works through the doctrines of Christianity, the Trinity, the Incarnation, prayer, the resurrection, as expressions of human wishes. Part II attacks their contradictions.
