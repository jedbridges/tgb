synopsis: >-
  One of the Monday Chats Sainte-Beuve wrote for the Paris press, dated October 1850. He asks what makes a writer a classic and answers that it is not conformity to ancient rules but the enrichment of the human mind in a form that is at once new and old, large and fine, easily contemporary with every age. He surveys Homer, Dante, Shakespeare, Goethe and the French seventeenth century, and imagines the classics as a temple of taste that keeps admitting new members.
