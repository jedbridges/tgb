synopsis: >-
  The middle play of the Oresteia. Years after Agamemnon's murder, his son Orestes returns from exile and meets his sister Electra at their father's grave, where she has been sent by Clytemnestra to pour libations after a frightening dream. Brother and sister recognise each other and, with the chorus of slave women, pray for vengeance. Orestes enters the palace disguised as a stranger bearing news of his own death, kills Aegisthus, and then, after a moment's hesitation, his mother. As he displays the bodies the Furies appear, visible to him alone, and he flees.
