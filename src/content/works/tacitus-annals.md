synopsis: >-
  Tacitus' history of the Julio-Claudian emperors from the death of Augustus in AD 14 to the last years of Nero, of which about two thirds survive. Books one to six cover Tiberius, the mutinies on the Rhine, the death of Germanicus and the rise and fall of Sejanus. The lost middle books covered Caligula and the beginning of Claudius. The surviving later books show Claudius ruled by his wives and freedmen, and Nero murdering his mother, the burning of Rome, the persecution of the Christians and the Pisonian conspiracy.
