synopsis: >-
  A dialogue in three books between Augustine and his friend Evodius on where evil comes from. Book I argues that wrongdoing arises from the will's turning from eternal to temporal goods, not from God. Book II proves the existence of God from the truths the mind discovers above itself, and argues that free will is a good even though it can be misused. Book III asks how foreknowledge is compatible with freedom and why God allows sin.
