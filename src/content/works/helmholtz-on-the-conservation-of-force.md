synopsis: >-
  A popular lecture introducing the principle Helmholtz had first stated mathematically in 1847. He starts from the long failure of attempts to build a perpetual motion machine and argues that this failure is not accidental but expresses a law: the quantity of force, what we now call energy, in an isolated system cannot be increased or destroyed, only converted. He traces the conversions through falling weights, springs, heat, electricity and chemical action, gives the mechanical equivalent of heat, and applies the principle to the steam engine, to animal bodies fed by food and to the sun's supply of heat to the earth.
