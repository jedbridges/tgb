synopsis: >-
  Othello, a Moorish general in the service of Venice, secretly marries Desdemona, a senator's daughter, and is posted to Cyprus against the Turks. His ensign Iago, passed over for promotion, sets out to destroy him. With a stolen handkerchief and a series of staged half-truths he persuades Othello that Desdemona is sleeping with the lieutenant Cassio. Othello smothers her in their bed, learns the truth from Iago's wife Emilia, and kills himself.
