synopsis: >-
  Based on Heisenberg's Gifford Lectures at St Andrews in 1955 and 1956 and written in English, the book explains quantum theory for non-specialists and draws out its consequences for philosophy. He recounts the history of the theory, sets out the Copenhagen interpretation in which the act of observation cannot be separated from what is observed, compares it with Greek atomism, Descartes and Kant, discusses the problem of language in describing atoms, surveys relativity and the criticisms of the Copenhagen view, and closes on the place of modern physics in human thought.
