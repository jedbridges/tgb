  Twelve scenes spanning 1624 to 1636, during the Thirty Years' War. Anna Fierling, called Mother Courage, follows the armies with a canteen wagon and her three children, selling to whichever side is paying. She haggles too long over a bribe while her son Swiss Cheese is shot, sees her other son executed for looting in a moment of peace, and loses her mute daughter Kattrin, who is killed drumming on a roof to warn a town of a night attack. At the end she harnesses herself to the wagon alone and follows the army again.
