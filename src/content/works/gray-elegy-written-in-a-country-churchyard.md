synopsis: >-
  A poem of thirty-two quatrains, probably begun at Stoke Poges in Buckinghamshire and finished after several years. At dusk in a village churchyard the speaker reflects on the obscure dead beneath the turf, who might have been poets or statesmen if poverty had not repressed their noble rage, and on how their unmarked lives were spared both the crimes and the glories of the great. It ends with the speaker imagining his own death and the epitaph a passer-by would read. It was for two centuries the most quoted poem in English.
