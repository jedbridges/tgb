synopsis: >-
  A selection of Leibniz's philosophical writings from the mid 1680s to his death, as gathered in the Ariew and Garber anthology used by St. John's College. Leibniz never wrote a single systematic book, and his philosophy has to be reconstructed from short essays, letters and summaries. The collection includes the Discourse on Metaphysics, the correspondence with Arnauld, papers on the nature of body and force, essays on freedom and contingency, the Principles of Nature and Grace, and the Monadology.
