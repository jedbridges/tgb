synopsis: >-
  In the mill town of Coketown, Thomas Gradgrind raises his children on facts alone and forbids imagination. His daughter Louisa is married off to the blustering manufacturer Bounderby and nearly seduced by an idle gentleman; his son Tom robs the bank and lets the honest weaver Stephen Blackpool take the blame. The circus people Gradgrind despised are the ones who save his son. The novel is Dickens's attack on utilitarian education and the factory system.
