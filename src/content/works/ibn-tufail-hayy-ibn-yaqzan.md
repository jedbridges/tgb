synopsis: >-
  A philosophical tale by the Andalusian physician and vizier Ibn Tufayl. Hayy, a child alone on an equatorial island, is suckled by a doe and, after her death, dissects her to find what has gone. Over seven-year stages he discovers fire, anatomy, the order of nature, the existence of a necessary being and finally a mystical union with God, all without language or teachers. When a religious man from a neighbouring island arrives, Hayy finds that his reason agrees with revelation but that most people cannot follow either, and he returns to his island.
