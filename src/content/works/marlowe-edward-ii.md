synopsis: >-
  Marlowe's history play opens with the new king recalling his favourite Gaveston from exile. The barons, led by Mortimer and joined at last by the neglected Queen Isabella, force Gaveston out, bring him back, and then capture and kill him. Edward raises new favourites and briefly wins, executing his enemies, until Isabella and Mortimer return from France with an army, depose him and put his young son on the throne. Edward is moved from prison to prison, made to give up his crown, and murdered in a cellar at Killingworth by the assassin Lightborn. The boy king Edward the Third then turns on Mortimer and his mother.
