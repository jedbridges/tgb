synopsis: >-
  After Oedipus' sons kill each other fighting for Thebes, the new king Creon decrees that Polynices, who attacked the city, must lie unburied. His niece Antigone buries the body anyway, appealing to the unwritten laws of the gods, and is condemned to be walled up alive. Creon's son Haemon, her betrothed, and the prophet Tiresias fail to move him until too late; Antigone hangs herself, Haemon and Creon's wife follow, and Creon is left alive with nothing.
