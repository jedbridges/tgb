synopsis: >-
  A two-volume study of what it has meant to be a woman. The first volume examines the facts and myths, arguing from biology, psychoanalysis, historical materialism, history and literature that woman has been defined as the Other in relation to man as the essential subject. The second follows a woman's life from childhood through sexual initiation, marriage, motherhood and old age, and considers the narcissist, the woman in love and the mystic before turning to the independent woman. Its best-known sentence holds that one is not born a woman but becomes one.
