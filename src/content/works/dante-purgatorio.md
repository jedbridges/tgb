synopsis: >-
  The second canticle of the Divine Comedy. Emerging from Hell at dawn on Easter Sunday, Dante and Virgil climb the mountain of Purgatory, where repentant souls are purged in turn of pride, envy, wrath, sloth, avarice, gluttony and lust before entering the Earthly Paradise at the summit. Along the way Dante meets the poets Sordello, Statius, Guido Guinizelli and Arnaut Daniel, and Virgil explains how love is the root of every virtue and vice. At the top Virgil vanishes and Beatrice appears to rebuke Dante and lead him on.
