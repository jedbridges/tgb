synopsis: >-
  A play in two acts, written in French in 1948 and 1949 and first staged in Paris in January 1953. Two tramps, Vladimir and Estragon, wait by a tree on a country road for a man called Godot, who does not come. They pass the time in talk, quarrels and routines, are visited by the master Pozzo and his roped servant Lucky, and each evening a boy arrives to say Godot will come tomorrow. The second act repeats the first with differences; the tree has leaves and Pozzo is blind.
