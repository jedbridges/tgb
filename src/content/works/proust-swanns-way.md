synopsis: >-
  The first volume of In Search of Lost Time, published at the author's expense in 1913. Combray recalls the narrator's childhood summers in a provincial town, his anguish waiting for his mother's goodnight kiss, and the taste of a madeleine dipped in tea that brings the whole past back. Swann in Love, a novel within the novel set before the narrator's birth, tells how the cultivated Charles Swann fell in love with the courtesan Odette, was consumed by jealousy, and married her after his love had died. A short third part, Place-Names, describes the narrator's boyhood love for their daughter Gilberte.
