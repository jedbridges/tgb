synopsis: >-
  Luther's response in the spring of 1525 to the Twelve Articles, the manifesto of the rebelling Swabian peasants. He divides the blame. The princes and bishops are told that their greed and tyranny provoked the rising and that God will destroy them if they do not relent. The peasants are told that their grievances may be just but that they have no right to take up the sword in the name of the gospel, and that a Christian suffers wrong rather than avenging it. He urges both sides to arbitration. Within weeks the revolt spread and Luther wrote a far more violent tract against it.
