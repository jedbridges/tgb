synopsis: >-
  The Geatish hero Beowulf sails to Denmark to rid King Hrothgar's hall Heorot of the monster Grendel, which has been killing his warriors at night for twelve years. He kills Grendel with his bare hands, then follows Grendel's mother to her lair beneath a lake and kills her too. Fifty years later, as king of the Geats, he faces a dragon roused by a stolen cup, kills it with the help of the young Wiglaf, and dies of its venom. The poem ends with his funeral pyre and a prophecy of his people's ruin.
