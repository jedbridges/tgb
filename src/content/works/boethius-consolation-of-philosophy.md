synopsis: >-
  Written in prison while Boethius, a Roman senator and scholar, awaited execution on a charge of treason under the Ostrogothic king Theodoric. Lady Philosophy appears in his cell and, alternating prose argument with verse, leads him from self-pity to understanding. Fortune's wheel turns for everyone; true happiness lies only in the good, which is God; evil has no real power; and God's foreknowledge, seen from eternity, does not take away human free will. The book was among the most read in medieval Europe.
