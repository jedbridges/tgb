synopsis: >-
  Written during the Depression, the book argues against the classical view that markets tend toward full employment. Keynes holds that the level of employment is set by aggregate demand, that saving and investment are brought into equality by changes in income rather than in the interest rate, that the rate of interest is governed by the preference for holding money, and that investment depends on expectations, animal spirits and the state of confidence. An economy can settle at an equilibrium with mass unemployment; the remedy is public investment and management of demand. Its six books are dense and its last chapter unusually plain.
