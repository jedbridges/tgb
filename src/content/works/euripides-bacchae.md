synopsis: >-
  The god Dionysus arrives in Thebes in human disguise to punish the city for denying his divinity. He drives the women of Thebes, including the royal family, into ecstatic worship on Mount Cithaeron. The young king Pentheus tries to suppress the cult, imprisons the stranger, and is then lured by him into spying on the women dressed as one of them. His mother Agave, in a god-sent frenzy, tears him apart with her bare hands and carries his head home believing it a lion's. Produced after Euripides' death, it won first prize.
