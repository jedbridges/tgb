synopsis: >-
  Thirteen books of definitions, postulates, common notions and propositions, each proved from what precedes it, compiled at Alexandria around 300 BCE and used as the textbook of geometry for two thousand years. Books I to IV treat plane figures, beginning with the construction of an equilateral triangle and reaching the Pythagorean theorem at the end of Book I. Book V gives Eudoxus's theory of proportion, Books VII to IX number theory including the infinitude of primes, Book X incommensurable magnitudes, and Books XI to XIII solid geometry, ending with the five regular solids.
