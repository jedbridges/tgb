synopsis: >-
  Written after the sack of Rome in 410, when pagans blamed Christianity for the disaster. Books I to X answer that charge, arguing that Rome's gods never protected it and that its history was one of lust for domination. Books XI to XXII tell the story of two cities, the earthly city founded on love of self and the heavenly city founded on love of God, from creation and the fall of the angels through Cain and Abel, Israel and Rome, to the last judgement and the peace of the saints.
