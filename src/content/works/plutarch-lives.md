synopsis: >-
  Forty-six biographies of Greek and Roman statesmen and soldiers arranged in pairs, a Greek matched with a Roman, most followed by a short comparison. The subjects run from the legendary founders Theseus and Romulus and the lawgivers Lycurgus and Solon through Pericles, Alcibiades and Alexander to Caesar, Cicero, Brutus and Antony. Plutarch says he is writing lives, not history, and looks for the small action or remark that reveals character, and for what a life teaches about virtue and vice.
