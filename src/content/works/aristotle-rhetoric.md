synopsis: >-
  Aristotle's treatment of persuasion as an art with its own method. Book I defines rhetoric as the ability to see the available means of persuasion, distinguishes deliberative, forensic and epideictic speech, and lays out the topics each draws on. Book II analyses the emotions and the characters of audiences a speaker must understand, and the enthymeme and example as rhetorical forms of argument. Book III treats style and arrangement.
