synopsis: >-
  The only complete Greek tragic trilogy to survive, first performed in Athens in 458 BCE. In Agamemnon the king returns from Troy and is murdered in his bath by his wife Clytemnestra, in revenge for the sacrifice of their daughter Iphigenia. In The Libation Bearers their son Orestes, commanded by Apollo, kills his mother and her lover. In The Eumenides the Furies pursue him to Athens, where Athena founds a court and casts the deciding vote for his acquittal, transforming the Furies into guardians of the city. The trilogy traces the passage from blood vengeance to law.
