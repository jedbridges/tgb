synopsis: >-
  Bacon's new logic for the sciences, offered as a replacement for Aristotle's Organon and published as the second part of his planned Great Instauration. Book I, in aphorisms, attacks the idols of the tribe, cave, marketplace and theatre that mislead the mind, and calls for knowledge built patiently from experiment rather than from syllogism. Book II sets out the method of tables and exclusions, using the nature of heat as its worked example.
