synopsis: >-
  The first constitution of independent Haiti, issued under Jean-Jacques Dessalines, who had been proclaimed emperor. It abolishes slavery forever, forbids any white man from owning property in Haiti, declares that all Haitians regardless of colour shall be known as black, establishes freedom of worship, and grants the emperor sweeping powers including the right to name his successor. It is the earliest modern constitution founded on the outlawing of slavery.
