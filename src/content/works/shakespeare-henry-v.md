synopsis: >-
  The reformed Prince Hal, now king, is persuaded by the Church that he has a claim to the French throne and invades France. A Chorus asks the audience to imagine the armies the stage cannot hold. Henry exposes three traitors, takes Harfleur, hangs his old companion Bardolph for looting, and walks in disguise among his soldiers the night before Agincourt, where his small and sick army destroys the French. The play ends with his courtship of the French princess Katherine and the treaty that makes him heir to France.
