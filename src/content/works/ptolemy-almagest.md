synopsis: >-
  The mathematical astronomy of the ancient world in thirteen books, written at Alexandria around 150 CE. Ptolemy assumes a spherical earth at rest at the centre of a spherical heaven, builds the trigonometry he needs including a table of chords, and then constructs geometrical models that predict the observed motions of the sun, moon and five planets using combinations of uniform circular motion: eccentric circles, epicycles and the equant. He treats eclipses, gives a catalogue of over a thousand stars in forty-eight constellations, and sets out the instruments and observations behind the models. It governed astronomy for fourteen hundred years.
