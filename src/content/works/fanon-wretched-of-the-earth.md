synopsis: >-
  Written in the last year of Fanon's life, during the Algerian war of independence, and published with a preface by Sartre weeks before he died. The opening chapter argues that colonialism is a system of violence and can only be ended by violence, which also remakes the colonised. Later chapters warn that a national bourgeoisie will betray independence, examine the role of peasants and the cities, and call for a new national culture. The last chapter presents case notes from Fanon's psychiatric patients, torturers and tortured alike.
