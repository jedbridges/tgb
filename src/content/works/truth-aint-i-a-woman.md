synopsis: >-
  A short speech Sojourner Truth, a formerly enslaved woman and travelling preacher, gave without notes at the Woman's Rights Convention in Akron, Ohio, in May 1851. She answered ministers who claimed women were too weak for rights by pointing to her own labour in the fields and the loss of her children, and argued from scripture that Christ came from God and a woman. The famous refrain comes from Frances Gage's 1863 reconstruction in dialect; the 1851 report by Marius Robinson reads differently.
