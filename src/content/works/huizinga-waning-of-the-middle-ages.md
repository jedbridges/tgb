synopsis: >-
  A study of life, thought and art in France and the Netherlands in the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries, published in Dutch in 1919. Huizinga presents the period not as the dawn of the Renaissance but as the overripe end of the Middle Ages, in which the forms of chivalry, courtly love, religious devotion and the cult of death had grown extravagant and hollow. He reads chronicles, poems and the paintings of van Eyck as evidence of a sensibility, not just a sequence of events.
