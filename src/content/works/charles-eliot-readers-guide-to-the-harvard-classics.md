  The apparatus volume of the fifty-volume Harvard Classics, the anthology Charles W. Eliot assembled after saying a five-foot shelf of books could give a liberal education to anyone willing to read fifteen minutes a day. It contains Eliot's introduction explaining the principles of selection, a reader's guide by William Allan Neilson arranging the contents by subject and by course of reading, a chronological index, an index of first lines of poems, and a general index. It is a map of the set rather than a work in its own right.
