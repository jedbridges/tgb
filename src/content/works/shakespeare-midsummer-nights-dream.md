  Hermia, ordered by her father to marry Demetrius, runs into the woods outside Athens with Lysander, followed by Demetrius and by Helena, who loves him. In the same woods Oberon and Titania, king and queen of the fairies, are quarrelling over a changeling boy, and a troupe of Athenian workmen are rehearsing a play for Duke Theseus's wedding.
