synopsis: >-
  A devotional handbook in four books, composed in a monastery near Zwolle and circulated anonymously before being ascribed to the canon Thomas à Kempis. The first book gives counsels for the spiritual life and warns against learning without humility; the second turns inward to the interior life; the third, the longest, is a dialogue between Christ and the disciple on consolation; the fourth concerns the sacrament of the altar. It has been the most widely read Christian book after the Bible.
