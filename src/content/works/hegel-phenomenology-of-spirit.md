synopsis: >-
  Hegel's first major book, an account of how consciousness rises through a sequence of shapes, from the bare certainty of the senses through perception and understanding, then to self-consciousness, where the famous struggle of master and slave appears, and on through reason, spirit, religion and finally absolute knowing. Each stage is shown to contradict itself and give way to the next. The book is at once an epistemology, a history of Western culture and an introduction to Hegel's system, and it is notoriously difficult.
