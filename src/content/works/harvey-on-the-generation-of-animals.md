synopsis: >-
  Harvey's last major work, published in London in 1651, records decades of dissection of hens' eggs at every stage of incubation and of deer from the royal parks in the breeding season. He describes the development of the chick day by day, argues against Aristotle and Galen that the embryo forms gradually by epigenesis rather than existing preformed, denies that the male semen contributes matter, and proposes on the title page that all animals come from an egg. The book includes discussions of the uterus, conception and the parts of the egg, and a long treatise on parturition.
