synopsis: >-
  Locke's attempt to map the limits of human knowledge. Book one denies that there are any innate ideas or principles. Book two argues that all ideas come from sensation and reflection and analyses simple and complex ideas, including the ideas of power, substance and personal identity, which Locke locates in continuity of consciousness. Book three treats language and the abuse of words. Book four asks what knowledge is, distinguishes it from probability and faith, and concludes that we know far less with certainty than we suppose.
