synopsis: >-
  An impoverished gentleman of La Mancha reads so many chivalric romances that he loses his wits, renames himself Don Quixote, and rides out as a knight errant to right wrongs, with a peasant neighbour, Sancho Panza, as his squire and a farm girl he has never spoken to as his lady Dulcinea. In Part One he attacks windmills, frees galley slaves and is brought home in a cage. In Part Two, published ten years later, he and Sancho meet people who have read Part One and stage adventures for them, Sancho governs an island, and the knight is defeated in Barcelona, returns home, recovers his sanity and dies.
