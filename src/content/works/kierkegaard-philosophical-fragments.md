synopsis: >-
  A short book issued under the pseudonym Johannes Climacus that asks whether an eternal happiness can be based on a historical point of departure. It sets against Socrates, for whom truth is recollected and the teacher only an occasion, a hypothesis in which the learner is in untruth, the teacher must be the god himself, and the moment of learning is decisive. Working out this hypothesis, Climacus arrives at the incarnation, the paradox that reason both seeks and cannot think, and argues that the disciple at second hand is no worse placed than the eyewitness, since faith, not knowledge, is what the paradox requires.
