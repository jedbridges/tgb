  Prince Myshkin, an epileptic returning to Russia from years in a Swiss clinic, is a man of complete goodness and candour whom Petersburg society takes for a fool. He is drawn between two women, the proud and damaged Nastasya Filippovna, who is being bought and sold among rich men, and the young Aglaya Epanchin. The merchant's son Rogozhin loves Nastasya with a violence that ends in her murder, and Myshkin, keeping vigil with the killer over her body, sinks back into idiocy.
