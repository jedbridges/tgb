synopsis: >-
  A short memoir of Thomas More by his son-in-law, who lived in More's household for sixteen years, written under Mary I as a source for a fuller biography. Roper records More's rise to Lord Chancellor, his conversations with Henry VIII in the garden at Chelsea, his refusal to swear the oath acknowledging the king's supremacy over the church, his imprisonment in the Tower, his trial on the testimony of Richard Rich, and his execution in 1535. The scenes of More's parting from his daughter Margaret at the Tower wharf were the source for the play A Man for All Seasons.
