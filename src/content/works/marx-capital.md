synopsis: >-
  Marx's analysis of the capitalist mode of production, of which only the first volume appeared in his lifetime; Engels edited the second and third from his manuscripts. Volume I begins with the commodity and the distinction between use value and exchange value, derives money and capital, and argues that profit arises from surplus value, the unpaid portion of the working day. Long chapters on the factory, machinery and the English working day document the history of capital's growth, and the book closes with primitive accumulation and the expropriation of the peasantry.
