synopsis: >-
  Six meditations conducted in the first person over six days. Descartes resolves to doubt everything that can be doubted: the senses deceive, dreams are indistinguishable from waking, and a malicious demon might be deceiving him about even mathematics. What survives is the fact that he is thinking, and so exists. From that single certainty he argues that he has a clear idea of a perfect God who cannot be a deceiver, that mind and body are two distinct substances, and that the material world can therefore be known through reason rather than the senses.
