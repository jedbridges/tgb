synopsis: >-
  A trilogy of plays, Homecoming, The Hunted and The Haunted, that transposes Aeschylus's Oresteia to New England at the end of the American Civil War. Brigadier General Ezra Mannon returns to his wife Christine, who has taken his cousin Adam Brant as a lover, and she poisons him. Their daughter Lavinia, who loves her father and hates her mother, drives her brother Orin to kill Brant; Christine shoots herself; Orin, consumed by guilt, kills himself; and Lavinia shuts herself up alone in the Mannon house with the dead. The whole runs some five hours in performance.
