synopsis: >-
  About eighty short works survive under the name of Lucian, a Syrian who wrote in Greek during the reign of Marcus Aurelius. Most are comic dialogues and essays that mock the gods, philosophers, historians and religious frauds of his day. The True History, a parody of travellers' tales, takes its narrator to the moon and inside a whale; The Sale of Creeds auctions off the philosophical schools; How to Write History is the only ancient treatise on historiography; the Dialogues of the Dead let Menippus jeer at the great in the underworld.
