synopsis: >-
  Troy has fallen. Outside its burning walls the captured women wait to be allotted to their Greek masters. The old queen Hecuba learns that her daughter Cassandra will be Agamemnon's concubine, that another daughter has been sacrificed on Achilles' tomb, and that her daughter-in-law Andromache goes to the son of the man who killed Hector. Then the Greeks decide that Hector's small son must be thrown from the walls. Helen is brought on and argues for her life. The play ends as the women are marched to the ships. Produced months after Athens massacred the island of Melos.
