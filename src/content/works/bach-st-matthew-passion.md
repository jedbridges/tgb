synopsis: >-
  A setting for soloists, two choirs and two orchestras of chapters 26 and 27 of Matthew's Gospel in Luther's German, first performed at St. Thomas's church in Leipzig on Good Friday 1727. The Evangelist sings the narrative in recitative, with Jesus, Peter, Pilate, Judas and the crowds taking their own words, and the story pauses for arias and chorales, on texts by Picander and from Lutheran hymns, in which the congregation reflects on what has happened. It runs from the plot against Jesus and the anointing at Bethany through the Last Supper, Gethsemane, the trial and crucifixion to the burial. St. John's reads it as a text in the sophomore seminar.
