synopsis: >-
  Not a book Luther wrote but one his students made. From 1531 the young men boarding in his house at Wittenberg took notes on what he said over supper, and the collections were gathered and printed after his death. The result is thousands of short entries, in a mixture of German and Latin, on scripture and predestination, on the papacy and the Turks, on music, marriage, his own melancholy and bowels, his enemies and his children. It is loose, funny, coarse and unguarded, and it is the closest thing we have to hearing him talk.
