synopsis: >-
  Kant's central work asks how knowledge that is both necessary and informative, such as mathematics and the principles of physics, is possible. His answer is that space and time are forms of human sensibility and that the categories of the understanding, such as causality and substance, are conditions that the mind imposes on experience. Knowledge is therefore limited to appearances, not things in themselves. The second half shows how reason, when it reaches beyond experience to the soul, the world as a whole and God, falls into illusion and contradiction, and clears room for faith and freedom.
