synopsis: >-
  A short metaphysical treatise written in English in the 1670s, surviving only in the Latin translation published anonymously in Amsterdam in 1690 and retranslated into English in 1692. Conway argues that there are three kinds of being, God, Christ as mediator, and creatures, and that all created things are one substance, a spirit-body continuum in which everything is alive and capable of rising or sinking in perfection. She rejects Descartes's dead matter, Hobbes's materialism and Spinoza's single substance, and treats suffering as purgative and restorative.
