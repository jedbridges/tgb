synopsis: >-
  Two Athenians, Peisetairos and Euelpides, fed up with lawsuits and taxes, leave the city to find the hoopoe Tereus and a quieter life. Peisetairos persuades the birds that they were the original gods and should build a walled city in the sky, Cloudcuckooland, to cut off the smoke of sacrifices from Olympus. Priests, poets, oracle-mongers and inspectors from Athens arrive and are beaten off. Starving gods send an embassy; Peisetairos negotiates Zeus's sceptre and a divine bride and ends the play as ruler of the universe.
