synopsis: >-
  Newton's account of his experiments on light, written in English and organised around what he did rather than what he proved. Book I shows by prism experiments that white light is a mixture of rays of different colours, each with its own refrangibility. Book II examines the colours of thin films, now called Newton's rings, and Book III the bending of light at edges. The book ends with the Queries, a series of speculations on the nature of light, matter, chemistry, gravity and God that shaped a century of science.
