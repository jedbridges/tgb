synopsis: >-
  A short introduction to Aristotle's Categories written by Plotinus's pupil Porphyry for a Roman senator. It explains the five predicables, genus, species, difference, property and accident, and how each is defined and related to the others. Porphyry raises but declines to answer whether genera and species exist in reality or only in the mind, the question that became the medieval problem of universals. Translated by Boethius, it opened the study of logic in every medieval school.
