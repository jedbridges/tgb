synopsis: >-
  Bergson's attempt to solve the problem of mind and body by rethinking both. He begins from images, of which the body is one, and argues that perception is not a picture in the brain but a selection from matter made in view of action. Memory is of two kinds, the habit memory stored in the body and the pure memory of the past which survives in itself and is recalled as needed. Evidence from aphasia and the recognition of objects is used to argue that the brain is an organ of action, not a store of thoughts.
