synopsis: >-
  The Herbert Spencer Lecture Einstein delivered at Oxford in June 1933, a few months after leaving Germany for good. In a dozen pages he argues that the concepts and laws of physics are free inventions of the human mind, not derived from experience by logic, though experience alone judges them. He credits Euclid and Galileo, defends the search for the mathematically simplest theory, and closes with his hopes and doubts about the new quantum mechanics and a unified field theory.
