synopsis: >-
  Dostoevsky's last novel. The dissolute landowner Fyodor Karamazov has three sons, the passionate soldier Dmitri, the intellectual Ivan and the novice monk Alyosha, and possibly a fourth, the servant Smerdyakov. Dmitri and his father compete for the same woman and quarrel over money. When Fyodor is murdered, Dmitri is arrested and tried, though the real killer was inspired by Ivan's argument that if God does not exist everything is permitted. The book contains the Grand Inquisitor parable and the life of the elder Zosima.
