synopsis: >-
  The first part of a trilogy in which Al-Farabi sets out the philosophy of Plato and Aristotle as a single project. It argues that the happiness of nations and cities depends on four things, the theoretical virtues, the deliberative virtues, the moral virtues and the practical arts, and describes how the philosopher who has mastered them must also be a legislator and a teacher who conveys the truth to the many through images. The true philosopher, king, legislator and imam are the same person.
