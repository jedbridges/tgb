synopsis: >-
  After a brief exchange with Socrates and the first telling of the Atlantis story, the Pythagorean Timaeus delivers a long account of how the cosmos was made. A divine craftsman, the Demiurge, shaped a disorderly receptacle into an ordered world after an eternal model, giving it a soul, a spherical body and the circling heavens as a moving image of eternity. Timaeus goes on to derive the four elements from triangles and to explain the human body, sensation and disease.
