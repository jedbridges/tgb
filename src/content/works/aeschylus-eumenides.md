synopsis: >-
  The final play of the Oresteia. Orestes, having killed his mother, takes refuge at Delphi, pursued by the Furies, ancient goddesses who avenge kindred blood. Apollo sends him to Athens, where Athena, rather than judge the case herself, founds the court of the Areopagus and summons citizen jurors. The Furies prosecute, Apollo defends, the votes are equal, and Athena's vote acquits him. She then persuades the enraged Furies to accept a home and honours in Athens as the Eumenides, the Kindly Ones, and the play ends in a torchlit procession.
