synopsis: >-
  Three connected essays on where our moral concepts came from. The first traces good and evil to a slave revolt in morality, in which the weak, driven by resentment, inverted the noble values of the strong. The second finds the origin of guilt and bad conscience in the creditor-debtor relation and the turning inward of cruelty once society forbids its outward expression. The third asks what ascetic ideals mean and argues that science and modern atheism are their latest form. Nietzsche's most systematic book.
