synopsis: >-
  A treatise on education cast as the story of an imaginary pupil, Emile, raised by a tutor from infancy to marriage. Rousseau argues that children should learn from things and experience rather than from books and precepts, that reasoning comes late, and that the aim is a man who is at once natural and fit to live among others. Book IV contains the Profession of Faith of the Savoyard Vicar, and Book V introduces Emile's bride Sophie and the education of women. The book was burned in Paris and Geneva.
