  Freud's late essay on why civilised life makes people unhappy. Society demands that individuals give up aggressive and sexual satisfactions, and the price is guilt, enforced from within by the superego. Beginning from the "oceanic feeling" of religion, Freud argues that the conflict between the drive toward love (Eros) and the death drive is built into culture itself and cannot be resolved, only managed.
