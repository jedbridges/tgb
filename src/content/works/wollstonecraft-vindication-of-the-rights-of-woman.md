synopsis: >-
  Wollstonecraft argues that women appear frivolous and weak not by nature but because they are educated to be, and that a society which denies them reason and independence corrupts both sexes. She attacks Rousseau's Émile and the conduct books that teach girls to please, calls for national coeducational day schools, and argues that women can only be good wives, mothers and citizens if they are first rational beings. Written in six weeks in 1791-1792, it is the founding text of liberal feminism.
