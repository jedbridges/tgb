synopsis: >-
  Twelve short narrative poems in Anglo-Norman French, written by a woman who names herself Marie and probably lived at the court of Henry II of England. She says she is retelling stories she heard from Breton minstrels. The lais tell of lovers separated by jealous husbands, a knight who is a werewolf, a lady who keeps a nightingale, a fairy mistress who carries her lover off to Avalon, and children abandoned and recovered, in a world where love is tested by loyalty and discretion rather than by law.
