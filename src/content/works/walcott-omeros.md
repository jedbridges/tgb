synopsis: >-
  A poem of some eight thousand lines in loose terza rima set mainly on Walcott's native island of St Lucia. Two fishermen, Achille and Hector, quarrel over Helen, a proud housemaid; Philoctete suffers from a wound on his shin that will not heal; a retired English officer, Major Plunkett, and his Irish wife Maud research the island's history; and the poet himself walks through the poem. Achille, in a sunstroke vision, travels to Africa and meets his ancestor; Hector dies in his taxi; Philoctete is cured by the healer Ma Kilman. The poem also moves to Boston, London, Dublin and the American plains, and ends with the sea going on.
