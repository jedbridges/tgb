synopsis: >-
  Phaedra, wife of Theseus, is consumed by a desire for her stepson Hippolytus that she regards as a curse from Venus. When Theseus is reported dead she confesses it; Hippolytus recoils, being himself secretly in love with the captive princess Aricia. Theseus returns alive, and Phaedra's nurse Oenone accuses Hippolytus of the crime. Theseus calls on Neptune to destroy his son, a sea monster does so, and Phaedra takes poison and tells the truth as she dies.
