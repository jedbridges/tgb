synopsis: >-
  Twenty-seven letters, first addressed to the Duke of Augustenburg and rewritten for publication in 1795 after the Terror had shaken Schiller's hopes for the French Revolution. He argues that political freedom cannot be achieved by people who are inwardly divided, and that modern specialisation has split the human being into a sense drive and a form drive. Only beauty, through the play drive, can reconcile them. Man is only fully human when he plays, and an aesthetic education is the necessary route to a free state.
