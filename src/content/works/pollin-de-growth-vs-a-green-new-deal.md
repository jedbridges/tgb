  An essay in New Left Review arguing against the degrowth movement, which holds that the climate emergency requires rich economies to shrink, and for a global Green New Deal. Pollin calculates that the emissions cuts needed cannot be reached by contracting GDP without mass unemployment, and that investing about 1.5 to 2 percent of global GDP a year in clean energy and efficiency could cut emissions while expanding employment.
