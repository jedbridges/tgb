synopsis: >-
  Socrates, challenged to show that justice is worth having for its own sake and not only for its rewards, proposes to find it first in a city, where it will be written large, and then in the soul. The city he builds in speech has three classes, a guardian class raised in common, philosophers as kings, and an education that begins by censoring Homer. The argument passes through the famous images of the sun, the divided line and the cave before returning, in the last book, to the question of what a just life is worth.
