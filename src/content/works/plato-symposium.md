synopsis: >-
  At a drinking party in honour of the tragedian Agathon, the guests agree to give speeches in praise of Eros, the god of love. Phaedrus, Pausanias, the doctor Eryximachus, Aristophanes and Agathon each speak in turn, Aristophanes with his fable of humans split in two and searching for their other halves. Socrates reports what the priestess Diotima taught him, that love is a ladder rising from beautiful bodies to Beauty itself, before a drunken Alcibiades bursts in and describes his failed seduction of Socrates.
