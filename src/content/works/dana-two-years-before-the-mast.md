synopsis: >-
  The journal of a Harvard undergraduate who, his eyes weakened by measles, shipped as a common sailor on the brig Pilgrim in 1834 for a voyage round Cape Horn to California, where the ship gathered hides along a coast then Mexican and nearly empty. Dana describes the work of the ship, a flogging by the captain, the hide trade at San Diego and Santa Barbara, and the return round the Horn in winter aboard the Alert. He wrote it to give a voice to seamen and to reform their treatment.
