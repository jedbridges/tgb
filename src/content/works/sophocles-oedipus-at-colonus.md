synopsis: >-
  Sophocles' last play, written in his nineties. The blind, aged Oedipus, led by his daughter Antigone, arrives at the grove of the Furies at Colonus outside Athens, where an oracle has said he will die. Theseus grants him protection. Creon tries to seize him by force and his son Polynices comes begging for his blessing; Oedipus curses both his sons. Summoned by thunder, he walks unaided to a death seen only by Theseus, and his grave becomes a blessing for Athens.
