synopsis: >-
  A narrative poem in the voice of François Bonivard, a Genevan prior imprisoned in the castle of Chillon on Lake Geneva in the 1530s. Chained to a pillar with his two brothers, he watches them sicken and die, and buries them in the dungeon floor. Left alone, he loses count of the years, is briefly consoled by a bird at his window, and grows so used to his chains that when freedom comes he regains it with a sigh. Byron wrote it after visiting the castle with Shelley in 1816.
