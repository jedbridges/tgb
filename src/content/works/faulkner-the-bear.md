synopsis: >-
  A novella of five parts from Go Down, Moses. Isaac McCaslin is initiated as a boy into the annual hunt for Old Ben, a huge bear in the Mississippi wilderness, under the tutelage of Sam Fathers, part Chickasaw and part Black. The bear is finally killed by the wild dog Lion and Boon Hogganbeck. In the long fourth section Ike, now twenty-one, reads the plantation ledgers, discovers his grandfather's incest and slavery, and renounces his inheritance. The last section returns to the vanishing woods.
