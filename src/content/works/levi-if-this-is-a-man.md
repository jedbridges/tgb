synopsis: >-
  Levi, a young chemist from Turin arrested as a partisan in December 1943, was deported as a Jew to Auschwitz in February 1944 and held in the Monowitz labour camp until the Russians arrived in January 1945. His account, written within a year of his return, describes the journey, the tattooing and stripping of the prisoners, the hunger, the work, the economy of the camp, the selections for the gas chambers, and the ten days between the German evacuation and liberation. He writes as an observer of what the camp did to human beings, including a chapter on reciting Dante's Ulysses canto to a fellow prisoner.
