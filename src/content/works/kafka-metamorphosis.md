  A novella of about sixty pages. Gregor Samsa, a travelling salesman who supports his parents and sister, wakes one morning to find himself changed into a monstrous insect. He cannot go to work; his family is first horrified, then burdened, and finally relieved when he dies. The story is told without explanation or metaphor, in a flat matter-of-fact prose that treats the impossible event as an ordinary domestic problem.
