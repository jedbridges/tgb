synopsis: >-
  Five books, the last of doubtful authorship, on the lives of two giants, Gargantua and his son Pantagruel. They eat and drink prodigiously, receive a humanist education in contrast to the scholastic one, fight comic wars, and found the Abbey of Thélème whose only rule is Do what you will. Pantagruel's companion, the rogue Panurge, cannot decide whether to marry and consults sibyls, poets, doctors and fools, and the later books follow their voyage past islands satirising lawyers, monks and papists to the oracle of the Holy Bottle, whose answer is Drink. The Great Books set prints the first four books.
