synopsis: >-
  An extended essay based on two lectures Woolf gave to women students at Cambridge in 1928. Its thesis is that a woman must have money and a room of her own if she is to write fiction. Woolf's narrator is turned off the lawn and away from the library of a men's college, compares the food at the men's and women's colleges, reads what men have written about women in the British Museum, and imagines what would have happened to a sister of Shakespeare with his gifts. She surveys women writers from Aphra Behn to Jane Austen and calls for an androgynous mind.
