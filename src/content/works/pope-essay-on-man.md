synopsis: >-
  A philosophical poem in four verse epistles in heroic couplets, addressed to Pope's friend Henry St John, Viscount Bolingbroke. It sets out to vindicate the ways of God to man by showing that the universe is an ordered whole, a great chain of being, in which apparent evil serves a good we cannot see. The epistles treat man in relation to the universe, to himself as a creature of reason and self-love, to society, and to happiness, and contain the lines whatever is, is right and the proper study of mankind is man.
