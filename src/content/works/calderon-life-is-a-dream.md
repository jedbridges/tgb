synopsis: >-
  Basilio, king of Poland, has kept his son Segismundo chained in a tower since birth because the stars foretold he would be a tyrant. He now has the prince drugged and brought to court for a day to test the prophecy. Segismundo wakes to power, throws a servant out of a window and threatens everyone, and is returned to the tower and told it was a dream. Freed by rebel soldiers he acts differently, reasoning that if life is a dream one should still do good, and he defeats and forgives his father. A parallel plot follows Rosaura's pursuit of the man who wronged her.
