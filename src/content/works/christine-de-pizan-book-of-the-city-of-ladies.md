synopsis: >-
  An allegorical defence of women written in 1405 in reply to the misogyny of clerical writers and the Romance of the Rose. Christine, despairing at what men have written about her sex, is visited by three crowned ladies, Reason, Rectitude and Justice, who help her build a walled city out of the lives of virtuous women drawn from Boccaccio, scripture and history, from Semiramis and the Amazons to Dido, Lucretia, the Virgin Mary and the saints. It is the first work in French by a woman to argue the case for women.
