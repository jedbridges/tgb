synopsis: >-
  In the twentieth year of the war with Sparta, the Athenian woman Lysistrata gathers the wives of Greece and persuades them to refuse sex with their husbands until the men make peace. The older women seize the Acropolis and the treasury. A magistrate who comes to reclaim it is dressed up as a woman and sent away. The strike holds despite desertions, the men of both sides arrive in visible distress, and Lysistrata brokers a treaty over the body of a naked girl called Reconciliation.
