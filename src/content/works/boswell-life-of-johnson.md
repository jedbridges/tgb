synopsis: >-
  A biography of the great critic, lexicographer and talker Samuel Johnson, written by the young Scot who attached himself to him in 1763 and recorded his conversation for the next twenty-one years. The early life is told from letters and secondhand reports; from Boswell's arrival on the scene the book becomes a diary of dinners, journeys and arguments, with Johnson's talk quoted at length. Garrick, Burke, Goldsmith, Reynolds and the members of the Club move through it. It is the most read biography in English.
