synopsis: >-
  A foundling is discovered in the bed of the benevolent Squire Allworthy and raised as Tom Jones alongside Allworthy's nephew Blifil. Tom, warm-hearted and imprudent, loves Sophia Western, daughter of a neighbouring squire who intends her for Blifil; Blifil's scheming gets Tom banished. Tom's journey toward London, Sophia's flight from a forced marriage, and their converging adventures at inns and on the road fill the middle books, set against the Jacobite rising of 1745. In London Tom sinks into an affair with Lady Bellaston and prison before his parentage is revealed and he wins Sophia. Each of the eighteen books opens with an essay by the narrator.
