synopsis: >-
  David tells his own life from birth. His widowed mother marries the cruel Murdstone; David is sent to a brutal school, then to label bottles in a London warehouse, lodging with the hopeful, penniless Micawber. He runs away to his aunt Betsey Trotwood, is educated, and becomes a writer. Around him move Steerforth, who seduces Little Em'ly; the fawning clerk Uriah Heep; his child-wife Dora; and Agnes Wickfield, whom he loves last. Dickens called it his favourite child.
