synopsis: >-
  Socrates' speech in his own defence at his trial in Athens in 399 BCE, on charges of impiety and corrupting the young. He explains how the Delphic oracle set him questioning the city's supposedly wise men, argues that a life without examination is not worth living, and refuses to abandon philosophy to save himself. Found guilty, he proposes a penalty of free meals for life, is sentenced to death, and tells the jurors why death is nothing to fear.
