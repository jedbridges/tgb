synopsis: >-
  A short treatise Bonaventure wrote on Mount La Verna in 1259, where Francis of Assisi had received the stigmata thirty-five years before. Taking the six wings of the seraph Francis saw as its plan, it leads the mind up in six steps, finding God first through the traces of Him in the visible world, then in the world as sensed, then in the image of God in the mind's own powers, then in that image reformed by grace, then in God's name as Being and finally as Good. A seventh chapter passes beyond the intellect into the darkness where the soul rests in God.
