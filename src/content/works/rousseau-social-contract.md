synopsis: >-
  Rousseau's treatise on legitimate political authority, opening with the line that man is born free and is everywhere in chains. It argues that the only rightful state is one founded on a social contract in which each person gives himself wholly to the community and so obeys only the general will, of which he is a part. The four books treat the contract, sovereignty and law, forms of government, and the institutions, including civil religion, that keep a republic alive.
