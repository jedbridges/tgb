synopsis: >-
  An account of the two years, two months and two days Thoreau spent from 1845 in a cabin he built beside Walden Pond, outside Concord, Massachusetts, compressed into the cycle of a single year. The long opening chapter, Economy, itemises the cost of the house and of his food and argues that most people work far more than they need to. The chapters that follow describe reading, sounds, solitude, visitors, the bean-field, the village, the ponds, winter animals and the thaw of spring, mixing close observation of nature with an argument for living deliberately.
