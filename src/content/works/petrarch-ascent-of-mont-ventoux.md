synopsis: >-
  A Latin letter to Petrarch's confessor Dionigi da Borgo San Sepolcro describing a climb of Mont Ventoux in Provence with his brother Gherardo on 26 April 1336. The brother takes the direct steep route while Petrarch keeps seeking easier paths and finds himself lower than before, which he reads as a figure for his own moral life. At the summit, overwhelmed by the view, he opens the copy of Augustine's Confessions he carries and lands on the passage rebuking men who admire mountains and neglect themselves. It is often called the first modern account of climbing for its own sake.
