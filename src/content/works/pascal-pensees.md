synopsis: >-
  The notes and fragments Pascal left at his death toward a defence of Christianity, found in bundles and first published in 1670. They range from a line to several pages and cover the misery and greatness of man, the diversions people use to avoid thinking about death, the limits of reason, the two infinities of the cosmos, the heart that has its reasons reason does not know, the evidence of prophecy and miracle, and the wager that a rational gambler should bet on God's existence. The order is uncertain and editions differ in their arrangement.
