synopsis: >-
  Two books on how things come into being and pass away in the region below the moon. Aristotle distinguishes generation proper from alteration and growth, criticises the atomists, and explains mixture, contact and action. Book II derives the four elements from pairs of the qualities hot, cold, wet and dry, shows how they change into one another, and traces the cycle of generation to the sun's movement along the ecliptic.
