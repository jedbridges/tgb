Plutarch was a Greek from the small town of Chaeronea who studied in Athens, visited Rome, held a priesthood at Delphi, and wrote the *Parallel Lives*, pairing a Greek with a Roman, Alexander with Caesar, Demosthenes with Cicero, to show character through action. Shakespeare took *Julius Caesar*, *Antony and Cleopatra* and *Coriolanus* from North's translation, and the founders of the American republic read him as a manual of virtue. The *Moralia* collect his essays on everything from the education of children to the face in the moon. Montaigne said he could not do without him.
