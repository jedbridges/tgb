Tacitus was a senator and consul under the emperors he described, married to the daughter of Agricola, and the greatest historian Rome produced. The *Histories* cover the civil wars of 69 and the Flavians; the *Annals*, written last, go back to Tiberius, Claudius and Nero, in a compressed, bitter Latin that makes every sentence an accusation. *Germany* uses the tribes beyond the Rhine as a mirror for Roman decay. Only fragments survive of both histories, and a single manuscript of each; Gibbon, who learned from him how to write about decline, called him the first of historians who applied the science of philosophy to the study of facts.
