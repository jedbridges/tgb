Arnold J. Toynbee taught at London and worked for the Foreign Office in both wars, and between 1934 and 1961 published the twelve volumes of *A Study of History*, comparing twenty-one civilisations to find the pattern of their rise through challenge and response and their fall through the failure of creative minorities. Somervell's abridgement made him, for a decade after 1946, the most read historian in the English-speaking world, and *Civilization on Trial* the essays that put the ideas to the public. The profession never accepted him, and the argument about whether his pattern was discovery or imposition has not ended.
