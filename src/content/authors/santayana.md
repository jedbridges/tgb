George Santayana was born in Madrid, raised in Boston, taught philosophy at Harvard beside William James and Royce, and at forty-eight, on receiving a legacy, resigned and left America for good, living in England, Paris and finally a convent hospital in Rome. *The Life of Reason* is the five-volume naturalist account of how reason grows out of instinct; *Skepticism and Animal Faith* the later, more austere system; *Persons and Places* the memoir. A materialist who loved the Catholic Church as poetry, he wrote the most elegant philosophical prose in English, and a bestselling novel at seventy-two.
