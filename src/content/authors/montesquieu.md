Montesquieu was a Bordeaux magistrate and wine grower who sold his office to travel and write. *The Persian Letters*, in which two visitors from Isfahan describe France to their friends, was the comic hit of 1721 and the model for every later outsider's view of Europe. *The Spirit of the Laws*, twenty years in the making, examined how climate, commerce, religion and custom shape a nation's laws, and argued for the separation of powers that the American founders took from him. He was the most cited author in the debates over the Constitution.
