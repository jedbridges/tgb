Adam Smith was a professor of moral philosophy at Glasgow and a friend of Hume who wrote two books, each the founding text of a discipline. *The Theory of Moral Sentiments* explains morality through sympathy and an impartial spectator within; *The Wealth of Nations* of 1776 explains how self-interest, the division of labour and free exchange produce the wealth of a people, and how governments hinder it. The invisible hand appears once in each. He spent his last years as a customs commissioner in Edinburgh, and asked that his unfinished manuscripts be burned.
