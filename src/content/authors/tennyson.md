Alfred Tennyson was a Lincolnshire rector's son who lost his closest friend Arthur Hallam at twenty-four and spent seventeen years on the elegy, *In Memoriam*, that made him Poet Laureate in 1850 and the voice of Victorian faith and doubt. *Ulysses*, *Tithonus*, *The Lady of Shalott*, *The Charge of the Light Brigade* and the twelve *Idylls of the King* on Arthur's court are the rest of the *Poems* the lists assign. He was made a baron in 1884, the first poet so honoured, and asked that *Crossing the Bar* close every edition. His ear was the finest in English verse since Milton.
