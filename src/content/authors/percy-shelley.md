Percy Bysshe Shelley was expelled from Oxford for a pamphlet on atheism, eloped twice, was denied custody of his children, and left England in 1818 for Italy, where he wrote nearly all the poetry that matters and drowned sailing in the Gulf of Spezia at twenty-nine. *Adonais* mourns Keats; *The Cenci* is the one Romantic tragedy still staged; *A Defence of Poetry* ends with poets as the unacknowledged legislators of the world. Mary Shelley, who wrote *Frankenstein* on their travels, edited him after his death. The Victorians made him an angel; he was a radical.
