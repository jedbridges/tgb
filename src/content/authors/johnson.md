Samuel Johnson came to London from Lichfield with a play in his pocket and no money, wrote for the magazines for years, and became the dominant literary figure of his age: the *Dictionary* of 1755, compiled almost alone in nine years, the moral essays of the Rambler, the philosophical tale *Rasselas*, written in a week to pay for his mother's funeral, the *Preface to Shakespeare* and the *Lives of the Poets*. Boswell's *Life* preserved his talk and made him the best-known Englishman of the century. He is read for the prose, the judgment, and the sense of a mind at full stretch.
