John Dewey taught philosophy at Chicago and Columbia for half a century and was the most influential American thinker of his time on education, democracy and the theory of knowledge. He held that thinking is a form of doing, tested by its consequences, and that schools should be places of inquiry rather than recitation. *Democracy and Education* is the founding text of progressive education, *How We Think* its method, and *Experience and Nature* the metaphysics behind them. St. John's and the Great Books set assign him as the last word in American pragmatism.
