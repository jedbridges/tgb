Marcel Proust was a wealthy asthmatic who spent his twenties in Paris society and his last fourteen years in a cork-lined bedroom writing *In Search of Lost Time*, seven volumes and three thousand pages, of which *Swann's Way*, refused by every publisher and printed at his own expense in 1913, is the first. The novel is the story of a man recovering his life through involuntary memory, the taste of a madeleine dipped in tea, and it contains the century's fullest studies of love, jealousy, snobbery and time. He died in 1922 correcting the proofs of the fifth volume.
