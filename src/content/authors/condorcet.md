Nicolas de Condorcet was a mathematician, permanent secretary of the Academy of Sciences, and the last of the philosophes, who welcomed the Revolution, drafted a constitution and a plan for public education, and voted against the king's death. Proscribed by the Jacobins, he wrote in hiding the *Sketch for a Historical Picture of the Progress of the Human Mind*, the fullest statement of the Enlightenment's faith in progress, and died in a cell the day after his capture. The *Political Writings* the lists assign include it with his essays on the rights of women and against slavery, and his work on voting and probability.
