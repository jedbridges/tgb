Jean-Paul Sartre was a lycée teacher who became, after the war, the most famous intellectual in the world, the voice of existentialism from the cafés of Saint-Germain. *Nausea*, his first novel, gives the discovery of contingency; *Being and Nothingness*, written partly as a prisoner of war, the philosophy of freedom and bad faith; *No Exit* the line that hell is other people. He edited *Les Temps modernes* with Beauvoir, moved toward Marxism, and refused the Nobel Prize in 1964. Fifty thousand people followed his coffin through Paris.
