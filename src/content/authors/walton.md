Izaak Walton was a London ironmonger and then a linen-draper who retired at fifty on the outbreak of civil war to the country and to fishing, and wrote *The Compleat Angler*, the most reprinted book in English after the Bible and the *Pilgrim's Progress*. He had known Donne, whose parishioner he was, and Herbert's friends, and his *Lives* of Donne, Wotton, Hooker, Herbert and Sanderson are the first English biographies written from affection, and portraits of a church he loved through its suppression. The *Lives of Donne and Herbert* are the two the lists assign, and every reader of either poet reads them first.
