Arthur Schopenhauer was the son of a Danzig merchant who left him independent, and he spent his life in lodgings in Frankfurt with a poodle, writing against Hegel, whom he scheduled his Berlin lectures to compete with and lost to. *The World as Will and Representation* of 1818 holds that the thing in itself behind appearances is a blind, striving will, that life is therefore suffering, and that art, compassion and ascetic denial are the only escapes; it was ignored for thirty years and then made him famous. *Studies in Pessimism* is the selection of the late essays that Bailey Saunders made for English readers. Wagner, Nietzsche, Tolstoy, Freud and Wittgenstein all read him.
