James Joyce left Dublin in 1904 with Nora Barnacle and never lived there again, writing about nothing else from Trieste, Zurich and Paris. *Dubliners* is fifteen stories of the city's paralysis; *A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man* follows Stephen Dedalus out of Ireland's nets; *Ulysses*, one day in June 1904 told in a different style for each hour, was banned in Britain and America for a decade and is the novel of the twentieth century on most lists. *Finnegans Wake* took the last seventeen years of his life. He was nearly blind and always short of money.
