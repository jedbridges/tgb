Elisabeth of Bohemia was a princess of the exiled Palatine court at The Hague, daughter of the Winter King and granddaughter of James I, who read Descartes's *Meditations* and in 1643 wrote to ask him how an immaterial soul could move a material body. The *Correspondence* that followed, over seven years, is the sharpest examination Descartes's dualism ever received, and he dedicated the *Principles* to her and wrote *The Passions of the Soul* at her prompting. She became abbess of the Lutheran convent at Herford, where she sheltered Quakers and Labadists, and was visited by Penn.
