Emmanuel Joseph Sieyès and Thomas Paine were the two great pamphleteers of the revolutionary age, Sieyès the abbé whose *What Is the Third Estate?* of January 1789 answered, everything, and who lived to help Napoleon to power, and Paine the English staymaker whose *Common Sense* made the American Revolution and whose *Rights of Man* answered Burke. In July 1791, after the king's flight to Varennes, they debated in the Paris press whether France should become a republic, Paine arguing for one and Sieyès for a constitutional monarchy as the surer guard of liberty. The exchange is the clearest statement of the choice the Revolution faced.
