Friedrich Nietzsche was a professor of classical philology at Basel at twenty-four, resigned through ill health ten years later, and wrote his major books in a decade of wandering between Swiss and Italian boarding houses before collapsing in Turin in 1889 and spending his last eleven years insane. *Thus Spoke Zarathustra* announced the death of God and the overman; *Beyond Good and Evil* and the *Genealogy of Morals* traced morality to its origins in resentment and power. His sister edited *The Will to Power* from his notebooks. He is the philosopher the twentieth century could not stop arguing with.
