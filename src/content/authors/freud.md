Sigmund Freud trained as a neurologist in Vienna and, from his work with patients suffering from hysteria, built psychoanalysis: the theory that much of mental life is unconscious, that dreams and slips reveal it, and that talking can treat what it produces. *The Interpretation of Dreams* set out the method; the later works, from *Beyond the Pleasure Principle* to *Civilization and Its Discontents*, turned it on society, religion and death. He fled the Nazis for London in 1938 and died there. The original 1952 Great Books set gave him a whole volume, the only twentieth-century author so honoured.
