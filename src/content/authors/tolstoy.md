Leo Tolstoy was a Russian count who fought in the Crimean War, gambled, farmed, married, and wrote *War and Peace* and *Anna Karenina* in his thirties and forties. A religious crisis around 1880 turned him against the church, the state, property and his own novels; he took up the peasants' dress and work, wrote parables and tracts, and in *What Is Art?* condemned most of the art of his class. Pilgrims came to Yasnaya Polyana from all over the world. He left home at eighty-two and died at a railway station. *Hadji Murat*, his last fiction, was found among his papers.
