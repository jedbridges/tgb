Charles Augustin Sainte-Beuve was the leading French critic of the nineteenth century, the author of the weekly *Causeries du lundi* for twenty years, in which he examined every writer from Montaigne to his contemporaries through the man behind the work, his temperament, his circle, his life. *What Is a Classic?* of 1850 gives the definition the Harvard Classics print: a writer who has enriched the human mind, said something true for all time, and spoken in a style that is his own and everyone's. Proust wrote a book against his method, arguing that the self that writes is not the self that lives, and the argument has not ended.
