Johann Wolfgang von Goethe was famous across Europe at twenty-five for *The Sorrows of Young Werther*, spent the rest of his life as a minister and then ornament of the small court of Weimar, and wrote in every form: lyric, drama, novel, epic, autobiography, and treatises on colour and plant morphology. *Faust*, begun in his twenties and finished months before his death at eighty-two, is the German national poem. *Egmont* is the play Beethoven scored, *Hermann and Dorothea* the idyll of the Revolution's refugees, and *Poetry and Truth* the account of how he became himself.
