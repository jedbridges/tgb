Robert Browning was thought obscure for most of his life and lived in the shadow of his wife, Elizabeth Barrett, with whom he eloped to Italy in 1846, until *The Ring and the Book* made him, in his late fifties, Tennyson's only rival. His form was the dramatic monologue, in which a duke, a painter or a murderer talks and gives himself away: My Last Duchess, Andrea del Sarto, Fra Lippo Lippi. The *Poems* the lists assign collect them; *A Blot in the 'Scutcheon* is the stage tragedy from his early attempt at the theatre. Pound and Eliot learned their voices from him.
