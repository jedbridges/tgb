Charles Darwin sailed round the world as naturalist on HMS Beagle from 1831 to 1836, came home with the materials for a theory of evolution by natural selection, and sat on it for twenty years, gathering evidence and fearing the consequences, until Wallace's letter forced his hand. *On the Origin of Species* appeared in 1859 and sold out in a day. *The Descent of Man* extended the argument to human beings. The *Voyage of the Beagle* is the travel book that started it. He lived quietly in Kent with his barnacles, orchids and earthworms, and was buried in Westminster Abbey.
