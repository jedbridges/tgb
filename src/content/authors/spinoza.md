Baruch Spinoza was a Portuguese-Jewish lens grinder in Amsterdam, excommunicated by his synagogue at twenty-three for opinions never recorded, who lived quietly in Dutch towns, refused a professorship at Heidelberg, and published only one book under his own name. The *Theologico-Political Treatise*, issued anonymously, argued for freedom of thought and read the Bible as a historical document, and was banned everywhere. The *Ethics*, published by his friends after his death, proves in geometrical order that God and Nature are one substance and that freedom is understanding necessity. Novalis called him a God-intoxicated man.
