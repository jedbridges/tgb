Karl Marx was a Rhineland journalist driven from Germany, France and Belgium for his politics who settled in London in 1849 and spent the rest of his life in the British Museum reading room, supported by Engels, writing *Capital*. *The Communist Manifesto* of 1848, written with Engels, announced that all history is the history of class struggle; the *Economic and Philosophic Manuscripts* and *The German Ideology*, unpublished in his lifetime, show the theory of alienation and of history as the development of productive forces taking shape. No thinker on the lists has had more effect on the world outside them.
