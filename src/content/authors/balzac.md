Honoré de Balzac wrote some ninety novels and stories in twenty years, working through the night on coffee, always in debt, and died at fifty-one, months after marrying the Polish countess he had courted for eighteen years. He conceived them as one work, *The Human Comedy*, a complete natural history of French society from the Restoration to the July Monarchy, with characters recurring from book to book. *Père Goriot* is the usual way in, *Eugénie Grandet* the tightest, *Cousin Bette* the darkest. Marx said he learned more from Balzac than from all the economists.
