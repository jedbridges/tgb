Anonymous is the name the lists give to the works whose makers are unknown, and it covers more of the shelf than any single author: the epic of *Gilgamesh* from Mesopotamian clay, the *Bhagavad Gita*, the books of *Genesis*, *Job*, *Psalms* and *Ecclesiastes*, the Gospels of *Matthew*, *Thomas* and *Judas*, the *Qur'an* as its Muslim readers would not put it, *Beowulf*, the *Song of Roland*, the *Nibelungenlied*, the *Poetic Edda* and the Icelandic sagas, *Sir Gawain*, the *Arabian Nights*, the traditional ballads, and the *Mayflower Compact* and *Constitution* whose drafters chose to speak for a people rather than themselves.
