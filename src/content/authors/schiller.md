Friedrich Schiller was a regimental surgeon who fled the Duke of Württemberg after his first play, *The Robbers*, made him famous, and spent his short life as dramatist, historian, philosopher and Goethe's friend and collaborator at Weimar. *The Aesthetic Education of Man* argues that beauty is the road to freedom and that man is only fully human when he plays; *William Tell*, his last play, is the drama of a people's liberation. The *Ode to Joy* is his, and Beethoven's Ninth its setting. He died of tuberculosis at forty-five.
