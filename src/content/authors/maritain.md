Jacques Maritain was a French philosopher who, with his wife Raïssa, converted to Catholicism in 1906 under the influence of Léon Bloy and made Thomas Aquinas the ground of a philosophy for the modern world. *Art and Scholasticism* applied Thomism to the arts and influenced Eliot, Stravinsky and Flannery O'Connor; *The Degrees of Knowledge* is his major work on science, philosophy and mystical knowledge; *True Humanism* and *The Rights of Man* helped shape Christian democracy and the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, on which he worked. He taught at Princeton and served as French ambassador to the Vatican.
