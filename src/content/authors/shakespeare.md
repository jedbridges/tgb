William Shakespeare was a glover's son from Stratford who went to London, acted, and wrote for the company that became the King's Men, sharing in its profits and retiring prosperous. Between about 1590 and 1612 he produced some thirty-eight plays and the *Sonnets*, never publishing the plays himself; friends collected them in the Folio of 1623. The comedies, histories and tragedies were written for a working theatre, at speed, and the lists still assign more of them than of any other author. *Hamlet*, *Lear*, *Macbeth* and *Othello* are read everywhere; the rest, from *Richard II* to *The Winter's Tale*, follow close behind.
