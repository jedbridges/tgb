Franz Kafka was a German-speaking Jew in Prague who worked as a lawyer for a workers' insurance company, wrote at night, published a few stories, and asked his friend Max Brod to burn the rest. Brod refused, and *The Trial*, *The Castle* and the unfinished *Amerika* appeared after Kafka's death from tuberculosis at forty. *The Metamorphosis*, in which a salesman wakes as an insect, is the one he published himself. The novels describe a world of arbitrary authority and endless corridors that the century then built. Kafkaesque is the only adjective on the lists that everyone uses.
