Julian of Norwich was an anchoress walled into a cell attached to a church in Norwich, of whom nothing is known but what she wrote: that in May 1373, at thirty, dying, she received sixteen showings of the passion of Christ, and spent the next twenty years understanding them. *Showings*, or *Revelations of Divine Love*, exists in a short and a long text, and is the first book in English by a woman. Its teaching that all shall be well, that God is our mother, and that sin is behovely, is the most hopeful in medieval theology. Eliot put her words into *Little Gidding*.
