Abraham Lincoln was born in a Kentucky log cabin, had less than a year of schooling, taught himself law, and served one term in Congress before the debates with Stephen Douglas in the 1858 Senate race made him a national figure. Elected president in 1860, he led the Union through the Civil War, issued the Emancipation Proclamation, and was shot five days after Lee's surrender. The *Gettysburg Address* and the *Second Inaugural* are the shortest great political prose in English; the lists read them with the debates to see the argument about slavery and the Union at full length.
