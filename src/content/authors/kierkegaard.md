Søren Kierkegaard lived on his inheritance in Copenhagen, broke off his engagement to Regine Olsen for reasons he spent the rest of his life examining, and in a dozen years wrote under a series of pseudonyms the books that founded existentialism: *Either/Or*, *Fear and Trembling*, on Abraham's sacrifice of Isaac and the leap of faith, *Philosophical Fragments*, on how the eternal can enter time, and *The Sickness unto Death*. His last years were spent attacking the Danish state church. He died at forty-two, almost unread outside Denmark, and was discovered by the twentieth century as its own.
