René Descartes was a French gentleman, soldier and mathematician who settled in the Dutch Republic to think in peace and died in Stockholm, tutoring Queen Christina in the cold before dawn. The *Discourse on Method* and the *Meditations* began modern philosophy by doubting everything that could be doubted and rebuilding from the one certainty that remained, the thinking self. The *Geometry* joined algebra to geometry and gave us the coordinate plane. The lists read the *Meditations* with the objections his contemporaries raised and his replies, since the argument was public from the start.
