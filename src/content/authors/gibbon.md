Edward Gibbon was a sickly child who read everything, was sent to Lausanne to be cured of Catholicism, served as a militia captain and a silent member of Parliament, and on the steps of the Capitol in Rome in 1764, hearing friars sing vespers in the temple of Jupiter, conceived *The Decline and Fall of the Roman Empire*. Six volumes appeared between 1776 and 1788, carrying the story from the Antonines to the fall of Constantinople in a prose of balanced irony that made the triumph of barbarism and religion sound like a comedy. The chapters on Christianity caused a scandal that has not entirely died.
