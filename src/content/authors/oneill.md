Eugene O'Neill was the son of a touring actor, a sailor, a derelict and a tuberculosis patient before he became the first American dramatist of world stature and the only one to win the Nobel Prize. *Mourning Becomes Electra* of 1931 sets the *Oresteia* in New England after the Civil War, thirteen acts in one evening, with the Mannon family playing the house of Atreus. *The Iceman Cometh* and *Long Day's Journey into Night*, the play about his own family that he ordered withheld until after his death, are the late masterpieces. He wrote with a tremor and died in a Boston hotel, saying, born in a hotel room and died in one.
