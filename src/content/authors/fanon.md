Frantz Fanon was a psychiatrist from Martinique who fought for Free France, trained in Lyon, took charge of a hospital in Algeria in 1953, and joined the FLN in the war against French rule, treating the tortured and the torturers alike. *Black Skin, White Masks* analysed the psychology of colonial racism; *The Wretched of the Earth*, dictated as he was dying of leukaemia at thirty-six and published with Sartre's preface in 1961, argues that decolonisation is necessarily violent and that the new nations must avoid the trap of a national bourgeoisie. It became the handbook of liberation movements everywhere.
