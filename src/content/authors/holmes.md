Oliver Wendell Holmes was a Boston physician, professor of anatomy at Harvard, and the wit of the Saturday Club, whose *Autocrat of the Breakfast-Table* made him famous and whose son became the Supreme Court justice. *The Contagiousness of Puerperal Fever* of 1843 argued from the evidence that childbed fever was carried from patient to patient by doctors' hands, and that a physician who had attended a case should not deliver a woman for weeks, four years before Semmelweis and decades before the germ theory explained why. The obstetricians attacked him. The Harvard Classics print it as a model of medical reasoning.
