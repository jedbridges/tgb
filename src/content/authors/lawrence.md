D. H. Lawrence was a Nottinghamshire miner's son who eloped with his professor's German wife, was hounded out of Cornwall as a spy in the war, and wandered Italy, Australia, Mexico and New Mexico in search of a life that had not been killed by industry and mind. *Sons and Lovers*, *The Rainbow* and *Women in Love* are the novels; *The Prussian Officer* the story collection of 1914 whose title piece, of an officer's cruelty to his orderly, is the one the lists assign. *Lady Chatterley's Lover* was banned until 1960. He died of tuberculosis at forty-four.
