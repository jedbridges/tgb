William James, elder brother of the novelist, trained as a physician at Harvard and taught there for thirty-five years, moving from physiology to psychology to philosophy. *The Principles of Psychology* of 1890 founded the field in America and gave it the stream of consciousness; *The Varieties of Religious Experience* studied faith as experience rather than doctrine; *Pragmatism* proposed that truth is what works, and the *Essays in Radical Empiricism* the metaphysics behind it. He wrote the clearest prose of any philosopher on the lists, and Henry once said he was the novelist of the family.
