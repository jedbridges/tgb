The Bible is the collection of Hebrew and Greek scriptures that Jews and Christians hold sacred, written over a thousand years by prophets, priests, poets, historians and evangelists whose names are in most cases unknown or traditional. The lists assign it in parts: the law and history of *Exodus*, *Leviticus*, *Deuteronomy* and *Samuel*, the prophets *Amos*, *Isaiah* and *Jonah*, and the Gospels of *Luke* and *John*. No book has been read by more people, translated into more languages or argued over more, and none has shaped the rest of the shelf so thoroughly; Dante, Milton, Bunyan, Dostoevsky and King are unreadable without it.
