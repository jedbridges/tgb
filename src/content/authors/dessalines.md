Jean-Jacques Dessalines was born enslaved in Saint-Domingue, rose to be Toussaint Louverture's chief lieutenant in the revolution, and after Toussaint's capture led the army that drove the French out. On 1 January 1804 he proclaimed Haiti's independence, the first nation founded by a slave revolt, and the *Declaration* he issued renounced France forever. The *Constitution of 1805* made him emperor and declared every Haitian citizen, whatever their colour, Black. He was assassinated within two years. The lists have added him beside the American founders as a document of what liberty meant to those it had been denied.
