Henry Wadsworth Longfellow was professor of modern languages at Harvard and the most popular poet in the English-speaking world in the nineteenth century, the first American poet given a bust in Poets' Corner. *Evangeline* of 1847, the story of an Acadian girl searching for the lover she lost in the expulsion of 1755, told in hexameters, sold sixty thousand copies; *Hiawatha* and *Paul Revere's Ride* are the others everyone knew. His second wife died in a fire in his study. He translated Dante, and the Harvard Classics assign him as the American poet the age most loved.
