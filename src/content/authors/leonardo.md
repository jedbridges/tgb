Leonardo da Vinci was a notary's illegitimate son from a Tuscan village who trained in Verrocchio's workshop, served the Sforza in Milan, the Borgia, the Medici and Francis I, and painted the *Last Supper* and the *Mona Lisa*. He filled thousands of pages with mirror writing and drawings on anatomy, water, flight, optics, war engines and the structure of everything he saw, and published nothing. The *Notebooks* the lists assign are selections from what survives, and the passages on painting as a science and on the eye are the closest thing to his philosophy. Vasari said heaven sometimes sends one man who is the whole of humanity.
