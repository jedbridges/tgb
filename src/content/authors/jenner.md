Edward Jenner was a Gloucestershire country doctor who noticed that milkmaids who had caught cowpox did not get smallpox, and in 1796 inoculated a boy with matter from a milkmaid's sore and then, weeks later, with smallpox, which did not take. *The Three Original Publications on Vaccination* of 1798 and after report the experiment and its extension, against the opposition of the profession, and the word vaccine comes from the cow. Parliament voted him thirty thousand pounds; Napoleon released English prisoners at his request; smallpox was declared eradicated in 1980. The Harvard Classics print the papers among the founding documents of medicine.
