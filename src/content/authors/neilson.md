William Allan Neilson was a Scottish-born Shakespeare scholar, professor of English at Harvard, and president of Smith College for twenty-two years, where he built it into one of the leading women's colleges. He edited the *Harvard Classics* with Charles Eliot and, in 1914, assembled the *Lectures on the Harvard Classics*, the fifty-first volume, in which Harvard faculty introduce the set by subject, history, philosophy, poetry, drama, religion and the rest, so that a reader could find a way through the fifty volumes. It is the map the series drew of itself, and the lists assign it as such.
