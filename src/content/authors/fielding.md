Henry Fielding was a playwright whose satires on Walpole provoked the Licensing Act of 1737 that closed the theatres to him, so he turned to law and then, in answer to Richardson's *Pamela*, to the novel. *Joseph Andrews* began as a parody and became something better; *Tom Jones*, the story of a foundling's road to London and his inheritance, is the most perfectly plotted comic novel in English, with a narrator who never stops talking to the reader. As magistrate for Westminster he founded the Bow Street Runners, London's first police. He died in Lisbon at forty-seven, seeking a cure.
