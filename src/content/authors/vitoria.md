Francisco de Vitoria was a Dominican theologian and the prime professor of theology at Salamanca, who lectured from the *Summa* of Aquinas and founded the school of Spanish scholastics that revived natural law. He published nothing; his lectures survive in students' notes. *On the American Indians* of 1539 examined the titles by which Spain claimed the New World and found most of them void, and *On the Law of War* set out when war is just, and together they are the founding texts of international law, which Grotius acknowledged. Charles V asked him to stop discussing the matter, and he did not.
