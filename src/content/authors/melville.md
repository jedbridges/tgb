Herman Melville went to sea at twenty, jumped ship in the Marquesas, lived with the islanders, and came home to write bestsellers about it. *Moby-Dick*, dedicated to Hawthorne, ended his popularity; the public wanted more South Seas and got a whale, a mad captain and the whole of creation. He spent nineteen years as a customs inspector in New York, wrote poetry no one read, and left *Billy Budd* in manuscript at his death in 1891, when the *Times* obituary got his name wrong. The twentieth century found *Moby-Dick* and made it the American epic.
